谈判核心词汇

核心概念

商务谈判是一种通过对话和协商来达成互利协议的过程。在英语商务环境中，谈判的成败不仅取决于策略和准备，更取决于你能否精确地使用谈判专用词汇来表达立场、提出条件、做出让步和达成共识。

谈判词汇的掌握程度直接影响你在谈判桌上的信心和效果。模糊的表达可能被对方利用，而精确的语言则能帮助你清晰地界定边界、保护利益。本节系统梳理商务谈判中最常用的核心词汇和表达，按谈判阶段组织，帮助你在各个环节都能准确传达意图。

准备与开场词汇
谈判的准备阶段决定了你在谈判桌上的自信程度。以下是准备和开场阶段的关键表达。


设定谈判目标：


    

  • Our primary objective in this negotiation is to secure a three-year contract with favorable pricing terms.
    • 

  • We need to establish our walk-away point before entering the discussion.
    • 

  • Let's identify our must-haves versus our nice-to-haves.
    • 

  • Our BATNA is to continue with our current supplier, so we're negotiating from a position of strength.
    • 



开场建立基调：


谈判开场应建立专业、合作的基调：


    

  • Thank you for making time for this discussion. We're looking forward to finding a mutually beneficial arrangement.
    • 

  • Before we dive into the specifics, I'd like to suggest we start by sharing our respective priorities. This will help us identify areas of alignment.
    • 

  • I believe there's a great opportunity for both our companies here. Let's explore how we can make this work for everyone.
    • 



设定议程和规则：


    

  • I'd suggest we structure our discussion as follows: first, we'll each outline our key requirements; then we'll discuss the commercial terms; and finally, we'll address the timeline.
    • 

  • To keep things productive, shall we agree to discuss one item at a time?
    • 

  • I'd like to propose that we aim to reach agreement by the end of this session, or at least identify the key areas that need further discussion.
    •

提出与回应报价
报价是谈判的核心环节。提出报价和回应报价的措辞直接影响谈判走向。


提出报价/条件：


    

  • We'd like to propose a unit price of $15 for orders exceeding 10,000 units.
    • 

  • Based on our cost analysis, our opening position is a service fee of $50,000 per quarter.
    • 

  • We're prepared to offer a 12-month warranty as part of the standard package.
    • 

  • Our initial proposal includes free shipping for the first year.
    • 



回应报价：


接受：


    

  • That's within the range we were considering. We can work with that.
    • 

  • We're comfortable with those terms. Let's move on to the next point.
    • 



部分接受：


    

  • We can agree to the pricing, but we'd need to discuss the payment schedule.
    • 

  • The overall structure looks good, but we have a concern about the delivery timeline.
    • 



拒绝（礼貌方式）：


    

  • I appreciate the offer, but unfortunately that's outside our budget. Is there room for flexibility?
    • 

  • That's a bit higher than what we had in mind. Could you walk us through the cost breakdown?
    • 

  • We're not in a position to accept that, but I'd like to explore what alternatives might work for both of us.
    • 



反提案：


    

  • What if we met in the middle? We could do $12 per unit for a committed volume of 15,000 units.
    • 

  • As a counter-proposal, we'd suggest extending the contract to 24 months in exchange for a 10% discount.
    • 

  • Instead of a flat fee, would you consider a performance-based pricing model?
    •

让步与交换策略
让步是谈判中不可避免的环节。关键在于让步应该是有策略的、有条件的、递减的——而不是一味退让。


有条件的让步：


    

  • We're willing to reduce the price by 5%, provided that you commit to a minimum order of 20,000 units.
    • 

  • If you can agree to net-30 payment terms, we can include the premium support package at no additional cost.
    • 

  • We can be flexible on the timeline if you're willing to adjust the scope accordingly.
    • 



表达灵活性：


    

  • There's some room for negotiation on the pricing, depending on the volume commitment.
    • 

  • We have some flexibility on the delivery schedule. What timeline would work best for your team?
    • 

  • While the base price is firm, we're open to discussing bundled services that could add more value.
    • 



表达坚定立场：


    

  • I understand your position, but the quality standards are non-negotiable for us.
    • 

  • We've already made significant concessions on pricing. This is as far as we can go.
    • 

  • That particular clause is a deal-breaker for us. We'd need to find a different approach.
    • 



识别和使用杠杆：


    

  • Given the current market conditions, this pricing reflects a significant discount from standard rates.
    • 

  • We have several other parties interested in this opportunity, so we'd appreciate a timely response.
    • 

  • Our timeline is flexible, which means we don't need to rush into an agreement that doesn't fully meet our requirements.
    • 



达成协议与确认
当双方在关键条款上达成一致时，需要清晰地确认协议内容，避免后续的理解偏差。


总结已达成的条款：


    

  • Let me summarize what we've agreed on so far: the unit price is $13, with a minimum order of 15,000 units per quarter, and net-45 payment terms. Is that correct?
    • 

  • Just to make sure we're aligned: the contract term is 24 months, with a 90-day termination notice period, and an annual price review mechanism.
    • 

  • Before we move forward, I want to confirm the key terms we've discussed today.
    • 



处理未决事项：


    

  • We've made great progress today. The two remaining items — the intellectual property clause and the SLA penalties — will need further discussion. Can we schedule a follow-up for Thursday?
    • 

  • I think we're 90% there. Let me take the outstanding points back to my team and come back to you with a proposal by end of week.
    • 



正式确认：


    

  • We're happy to proceed on the terms discussed. I'll have our legal team draft the contract and send it over for your review.
    • 

  • This has been a productive discussion. I'll follow up with a summary email confirming all the points we've agreed on.
    • 

  • We have a deal. Let's get the paperwork moving. I'll send the draft agreement by Friday.
    • 



表达不确定性：


    

  • I need to run this by my management before I can commit. Can I get back to you by Monday?
    • 

  • In principle, we're aligned, but I want to make sure my technical team signs off on the feasibility.
    • 

  • We're tentatively agreed, subject to final approval from our board.
    •

小结
    

  • 谈判词汇的精确性直接影响谈判效果——模糊的表达可能被对方利用
    • 

  • BATNA、ZOPA 和保留价格是谈判准备的三个核心概念，必须在谈判前明确
    • 

  • 开场阶段应建立合作基调，使用"mutually beneficial"和"partnership"等框架
    • 

  • 报价和反提案应具体、有依据，避免空洞的数字——说明"为什么是这个价格"
    • 

  • 让步应该是有条件的（"If you... we can..."），递减的，且始终追求对等交换
    • 

  • 达成协议后立即口头总结并发送书面确认，防止理解偏差
    • 

  • 保留"I need to check with my team"的空间是合理的谈判策略，不是软弱的表现
    •
