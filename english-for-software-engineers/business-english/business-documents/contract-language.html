合同语言要点

核心概念

商务合同（Business Contract）是商业关系的法律基础。它规定了各方的权利、义务、责任和救济措施。合同语言是一种高度专业化的英语——它追求精确性、完整性和无歧义性，与日常商务沟通的简洁风格形成鲜明对比。

对于非法律专业的商务人士来说，完全理解合同的每一个条款可能不现实，但掌握合同的基本结构、核心术语和常见条款的含义是确保商业利益不受损害的基本要求。本节不是教你如何起草合同——那是律师的工作——而是帮助你理解合同语言的逻辑，识别关键条款，并在审阅合同时知道该关注什么。

合同的标准结构
一份标准的英文商务合同通常由以下部分组成。


Recitals（序言/鉴于条款）：


序言用 "WHEREAS" 引导，说明合同的背景和目的：




WHEREAS, the Company desires to engage the Contractor to provide software development services; and


WHEREAS, the Contractor has the expertise and capacity to perform such services;


NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the parties agree as follows:




Definitions（定义条款）：


合同中的关键术语需要精确定义，避免歧义：




"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by either party to the other, whether orally, in writing, or in electronic form, that is designated as confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure.






"Deliverables" means the work product, including all software code, documentation, and related materials, to be delivered by the Contractor to the Company as specified in the applicable Statement of Work.

审阅合同的关键条款
在审阅合同时，以下条款需要特别关注，因为它们直接影响你的权利和风险。


Scope of Work / Services（服务范围）：


这是合同的核心——明确定义你要做什么（或对方要为你做什么）。范围不清晰是合同纠纷的首要原因：


    

  • 关注点：交付物是否清晰列出？验收标准是否明确？变更如何处理？
    • 

  • 关键表达：The Services shall include... / The following items are expressly excluded from the scope of this Agreement...
    • 



Payment Terms（付款条件）：


    

  • 付款金额和币种
    • 

  • 付款时间表（milestone-based vs. time-based）
    • 

  • 逾期付款的后果
    • 

  • 关键表达：Payment shall be due within 30 days of invoice date. / Late payments shall accrue interest at a rate of 1.5% per month.
    • 



Term and Termination（期限与终止）：


    

  • 合同的起止日期
    • 

  • 自动续期条款（auto-renewal）
    • 

  • 终止条件：有因终止（for cause）vs. 无因终止（for convenience）
    • 

  • 终止通知期
    • 

  • 关键表达：This Agreement shall commence on [date] and continue for a period of 12 months, unless earlier terminated. / Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon 30 days' prior written notice.
    • 



Limitation of Liability（责任限制）：


这可能是合同中最重要的保护条款。它规定了一方对另一方可能承担的最大责任：




IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID OR PAYABLE UNDER THIS AGREEMENT DURING THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.






NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES.

合同协商的常用表达
在合同审阅和协商过程中，需要用到一套专业而外交的语言来提出修改意见。


请求修改：


    

  • We'd like to propose an amendment to Section 5.2 regarding the payment terms.
    • 

  • Our legal team has flagged a concern with the indemnification clause. Could we discuss an alternative formulation?
    • 

  • We're comfortable with most of the terms, but we'd like to negotiate the termination notice period from 30 to 60 days.
    • 



表达关切：


    

  • We have some concerns about the breadth of the non-compete clause. As currently drafted, it could restrict our ability to serve other clients in the same industry.
    • 

  • The liability cap in Section 8 seems disproportionate to the contract value. We'd suggest limiting it to the total fees paid under this Agreement.
    • 



提出替代方案：


    

  • Instead of a flat fee structure, we'd propose a milestone-based payment schedule to better align incentives.
    • 

  • As an alternative to the current termination clause, we'd suggest mutual termination for convenience with 60 days' notice and a reasonable wind-down period.
    • 



达成一致：


    

  • We're prepared to accept the revised terms for Section 4 as discussed.
    • 

  • Pending final review by our legal team, we're aligned on the key commercial terms.
    • 

  • Let's have our respective legal teams finalize the language and target execution by end of next week.
    • 



请求澄清：


    

  • Could you clarify what is meant by "reasonable efforts" in Section 3.1? We'd prefer to see specific performance metrics.
    • 

  • The definition of "Intellectual Property" in Section 1 seems quite broad. Does it include pre-existing IP that the Contractor brings to the engagement?
    • 



常见合同条款模板
以下是几个常见合同条款的标准语言模板，帮助你理解合同的典型表述方式。


保密条款（Confidentiality）：




Each party agrees to hold in strict confidence all Confidential Information received from the other party, to use such information solely for the purposes of this Agreement, and not to disclose such information to any third party without the prior written consent of the disclosing party. This obligation shall survive the termination of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years.




不可抗力条款（Force Majeure）：




Neither party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performing its obligations under this Agreement to the extent that such failure or delay results from circumstances beyond the reasonable control of the affected party, including but not limited to acts of God, natural disasters, war, terrorism, riots, epidemics, government actions, or internet service provider failures.




争议解决条款（Dispute Resolution）：




Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall first be submitted to good faith negotiation between the parties. If the dispute cannot be resolved through negotiation within thirty (30) days, it shall be submitted to binding arbitration administered by [Arbitration Body] in accordance with its rules, with the proceedings to be held in [City, Country].




小结
    

  • 合同语言追求精确性和无歧义性，与日常商务沟通风格有本质区别
    • 

  • "shall"表示义务（必须做），"may"表示权利（可以做）——混淆会导致严重后果
    • 

  • 审阅合同时重点关注五个条款：服务范围、付款条件、期限终止、责任限制、知识产权
    • 

  • 合同协商需要用专业而外交的语言——既要坚持底线，又要维护合作关系
    • 

  • 模糊的服务范围是合同纠纷的首要原因，确保交付物和验收标准被精确定义
    • 

  • 责任限制条款可能是合同中最重要的保护条款，关注责任上限和免责范围
    • 

  • 合同审阅不能替代法律建议——重要合同务必由专业律师审核
    •
