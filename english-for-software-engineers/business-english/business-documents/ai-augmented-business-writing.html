AI 辅助商务写作

AI 工具（Claude / GPT / Copilot for Business / Gemini）已成为商务写作的常规副手。但 AI 起草的商务英语有显著"AI 味"——过度礼貌、模糊修饰、缺乏具体细节、文化适配迟钝。本文聚焦：哪些商务文档适合 AI 辅助、prompt 模式按文档类型分化、AI 味 的 5 类信号与修整、跨文化语气调整。

哪些商务写作适合 AI 辅助

不是所有商务写作都该 AI 起草。决策矩阵：

文档类型 AI 起草 ROI 原因
内部周报 / 状态更新 ★★★★★ 模板化强，AI 输出经轻调即可用
客户邮件（首封冷邮件） ★★ 个性化是核心，AI 通用模板易暴露
客户邮件（复杂场景回复） ★★★★ AI 帮 brainstorming 句式，人定细节
提案 / Proposal ★★★★ 结构 AI 给，内容人补
合同 / 法律文书 ★ 法律责任高，AI 易引入风险性表达
内部备忘录（memo） ★★★★★ 模板化，且不出公司，风险低
绩效评估 / 1:1 反馈 ★★ 个人化 + 情感敏感，AI 容易显得机械
跨文化道歉信 / 危机沟通 ★ 语气掌控极敏感，AI 失误代价高

原则：内部 + 模板化 → AI 高 ROI；外部 + 个性化 → AI 仅辅助 brainstorming。

按文档类型的 prompt 模式

1. 周报 / 状态更新

You're drafting my weekly status update for an engineering team.

Input data:
- Shipped this week: [list bullets]
- In progress: [list bullets]
- Blocked: [list bullets]
- Plans for next week: [list bullets]

Format:
- Start with one-line summary (no greeting)
- Use the section structure Shipped / In flight / Blocked / Next week
- Plain prose under each header, not nested bullets
- Mention specific people with @ when assigning follow-ups
- End with a one-line CTA (e.g., "Drop comments in 🧵")

Style:
- Active voice, past tense for shipped, present continuous for in-flight
- No "I am pleased to inform" / "I would like to share" — straight to it
- Under 200 words total

2. 客户邮件（复杂回复）

You're drafting a response to a customer email. Here is the email I received:
[paste]

My intended message: [短中文或英文要点]

Constraints:
- Acknowledge their concern in the first sentence
- Address each point they raised, in order
- Be concrete with timelines (use dates, not "soon")
- Avoid "I sincerely apologize for the inconvenience" — say what's
  being done instead
- If declining anything, give one clear reason, not three weak ones
- Sign off with a specific next step they can hold us to

Target length: 100-150 words. Plain English; no "we will endeavor to"
type phrasing.

3. Proposal / 提案

You're drafting a B2B proposal section for {topic}.

Audience: [decision-maker title + company context]
Goal: [what we want them to do]

Section to draft: [Executive Summary | Approach | Pricing | Timeline]

Constraints:
- Use 1-2 paragraphs + 1 bulleted list per section
- Quantify impact wherever possible ("reduce processing time by X%")
- Avoid generic claims ("we are committed to excellence")
- Match the audience's vocabulary (technical for CTO, business for CFO)
- Use "you" / "your team" for the customer, "we" / "our team" for us

4. 内部 memo

Write an internal memo announcing {change}.

Context: [why this is happening, what was decided]

Memo structure:
1. One-sentence summary in **bold** at top
2. What's changing (1-2 paragraphs)
3. What it means for the team (3-5 bullets)
4. Timeline / next steps (numbered list with dates)
5. Where to ask questions (Slack channel or person)

Style:
- Plain English, no corporate speak ("synergize", "leverage")
- Acknowledge if this change is controversial; don't pretend it isn't
- Under 300 words

AI 味的 5 类信号

AI 起草的商务英语有可识别的"指纹"。修整阶段重点检查：

1. 过度礼貌的开场白

# AI 默认
"I hope this email finds you well. I am writing to..."

# 修整后
"Following up on Tuesday's discussion —"
"Quick question on the Q3 budget —"
"Sharing the contract draft for your review —"

母语商务英语已经 70% 删除了"I hope this email finds you well"。仅在第一次接触 + 较正式语境保留。

2. 模糊的修饰词

AI 易写 修整后
"significantly improve" "reduce processing time from 4 days to 6 hours"
"industry-leading solution" "the same approach used by Stripe and Shopify"
"robust and scalable" "handles 50K req/min with p99 < 200ms"
"best-in-class" (删除——空话)
"deep expertise" "5 years building payment infrastructure at $COMPANY"

公式：模糊形容词 → 具体数字 / 名字 / 时间。

3. 自我引用循环

AI 在结尾常出现重复总结：

# AI 易写
"In summary, this proposal outlines our approach to..."
"As described above..."
"As I mentioned earlier..."

# 修整
直接删除——读者刚读完，不需要被告知"刚读完"。

4. 三段排比的滥用

AI 偏爱"三个并列形容词 / 名词 / 动词"句式：

# AI 易写
"We bring innovation, expertise, and dedication to every project."
"Our solution is fast, reliable, and cost-effective."

# 修整
"We've shipped 3 fintech platforms in the last 2 years."
"Our solution: 200ms latency, 99.95% uptime, $0.001 per transaction."

三段排比在公关稿可以，在 B2B 提案里读起来像 ChatGPT 4.0 默认风格。

5. 文化迟钝

AI 默认按美国商务英语风格输出。跨文化场景要主动调整：

文化 AI 默认（美式） 调整方向
日本 "Let me know what works for you." "Please advise at your convenience."
德国 "Hope to hear from you soon!" "Looking forward to your formal response by 15 May."
英国 "Awesome! Reach out anytime." "Many thanks. Do let me know if anything is unclear."
北欧 "Excited to chat further!" "Available for a call next Tuesday or Wednesday."
中东 太直接 在开头加一句关系性问候

在 prompt 中显式声明对方文化背景：The recipient is a German CFO; tone should be formal, structured, and avoid casual exclamations.

AI 辅助的 3 步修整流程

不要直接发送 AI 输出。3 步修整：

Step 1 — 删（去除 AI 味）

  • 删除所有 "I hope this finds you well" / "Hope you're doing well" 开头
  • 删除 "Please let me know if you have any questions" 兜底句（除非真的需要）
  • 删除三段排比中最弱的一段
  • 删除模糊形容词，替换为具体数据

Step 2 — 加（补上 AI 不知道的）

  • 加上具体的人名、项目名、日期
  • 加上一句你的判断 / 推荐（AI 倾向中立陈述）
  • 加上一个对方可立即采取的行动 / 反应通道

Step 3 — 调（语气与文化适配）

  • 跨文化场景：参考上表调整语气强度
  • 长期合作关系：加一句关系性问候（不限于开头）
  • 内部 vs 外部：内部可更随意，外部更精细

常用 AI 辅助 prompt 提示词

把这些纳入你的 prompt 库：

Prompt 提示词 作用
Plain English, no corporate jargon 抑制 "synergize / leverage / circle back"
Quantify impact wherever possible 强制具体化
Match the recipient's level: {C-level / IC / non-technical} 调整术语密度
Avoid three-item parallel structures 抑制三段排比
Active voice in all sentences 抑制被动语态泛滥
One concrete next step at the end 强制 CTA 化结尾
Tone: {direct / formal / warm / urgent} 显式控制语气
Do not start with "I hope..." or "Hope you're..." 显式删冗开场

AI 与"署名"的伦理

AI 起草后由人发送，应不应该披露？商务场景共识正在形成：

场景 是否披露
内部周报 / 状态更新 不必披露（工具属性）
客户邮件（你的署名） 应当亲手修整，不必特别披露
提案 / RFP 必须深度参与，AI 仅辅助
法律文书 / 合同 绝不直接采用 AI 输出
文学性 / 个人性强的（道歉、致谢） 不应用 AI 起草——读者会感觉到

底线：发出去的文字要能为之负责。AI 起草不解除你的判断责任。

小结

  • AI 辅助商务写作的 ROI 取决于文档类型——内部 + 模板化高，外部 + 个性化低
  • 不同文档类型有不同的 prompt 模式（周报 / 客户邮件 / 提案 / memo）
  • AI 味的 5 类信号：过度礼貌开场、模糊修饰词、自我引用循环、三段排比、文化迟钝
  • 3 步修整流程：删（去除 AI 味）→ 加（补具体细节）→ 调（语气文化适配）
  • 跨文化场景在 prompt 中显式声明对方文化背景
  • AI 起草不解除判断责任——发出去的文字要能为之负责
