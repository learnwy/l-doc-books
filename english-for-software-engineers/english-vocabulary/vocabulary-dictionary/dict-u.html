词典 U

职场与商务

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配 例句
undertake v. 承担，着手做 undertake a project, undertake responsibility She agreed to undertake the new initiative.
utilize v. 利用，使用 fully utilize, utilize resources We should utilize existing tools before building new ones.
unanimous adj. 一致同意的 unanimous decision, unanimous vote The board reached a unanimous decision.
urgent adj. 紧急的 urgent matter, urgent request Please handle this urgent request first.
update n./v. 更新；使更新 status update, update the report I'll send a status update after the meeting.
upfront adj./adv. 预先的；坦率地 upfront cost, be upfront about Let me be upfront about the risks involved.
undermine v. 逐渐破坏，削弱 undermine confidence, undermine authority Constant criticism can undermine team morale.
unveil v. 揭露，公布 unveil a plan, unveil a product The CEO will unveil the new strategy at the summit.
uphold v. 维护，支持 uphold standards, uphold the policy We must uphold high standards of quality.
underscore v. 强调，突出 underscore the importance This incident underscores the need for better training.
unilateral adj. 单方面的 unilateral decision, unilateral action The manager made a unilateral decision without consulting the team.

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配 例句
underestimate v. 低估 underestimate the difficulty Don't underestimate how long the move will take.
ultimately adv. 最终地 ultimately decide, ultimately responsible Ultimately, the choice is yours to make.
unique adj. 独特的 unique style, unique opportunity Each person has a unique perspective.
unprecedented adj. 前所未有的 unprecedented growth, unprecedented challenge The pandemic created unprecedented disruption.
unwrap v. 打开（包装），拆开 unwrap a gift, unwrap the package The kids couldn't wait to unwrap their presents.
upbeat adj. 乐观的，积极的 upbeat attitude, upbeat mood She remained upbeat despite the setback.
ubiquitous adj. 无处不在的 ubiquitous technology, ubiquitous presence Smartphones have become ubiquitous in daily life.
unwind v. 放松，解压 unwind after work, unwind with music I like to unwind with a good book in the evening.
utter adj./v. 完全的；说出 utter surprise, utter a word She stared in utter disbelief at the result.
upside n. 好的一面，优点 the upside is, upside potential The upside of working remotely is saving commute time.
unconditional adj. 无条件的 unconditional love, unconditional support A parent's love for their child is unconditional.

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配 例句

易混淆辨析

词组 区别说明
utilize vs. use utilize 更正式，暗示充分有效利用；use 更通用日常
update vs. upgrade update 指小版本更新或信息刷新，upgrade 指大版本升级或质量提升
unique vs. special unique 强调独一无二，special 强调与众不同或受到特别对待
urgent vs. important urgent 强调时间紧迫，important 强调事情重要但未必紧急
undermine vs. weaken undermine 含暗中破坏之意，weaken 只表示使变弱
underscore vs. emphasize underscore 更书面，常用于正式场合；emphasize 更通用
