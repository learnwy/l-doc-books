词典 L

本页收录以字母 L 开头的实用词汇，涵盖职场、日常和技术三大场景。

职场与商务

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配 例句
launch v./n. 发布；启动 product launch The product launch is planned for next Friday.
leverage v./n. 利用；杠杆 leverage resources We should leverage existing tools to save time.
lead v./n. 领导；主导 lead a team She leads a cross-functional team of twelve people.
liability n. 责任；负债 legal liability The company faces potential legal liability for the breach.
layout n. 布局；安排 office layout The new office layout encourages open collaboration.
lucrative adj. 利润丰厚的 lucrative deal They signed a lucrative deal with the overseas client.
lean adj./v. 精益的；倾斜 lean methodology We adopted lean methodology to reduce waste in our process.
liaison n. 联络人；联系 act as liaison She acts as liaison between the engineering and sales teams.
logistics n. 物流；后勤 handle logistics The operations team handles all event logistics.

日常与社交

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配 例句
leisure n. 闲暇；休闲 at leisure / leisure time I enjoy reading during my leisure time.
lively adj. 活泼的；生动的 lively discussion The meetup turned into a lively discussion about AI.
loyal adj. 忠诚的；忠实的 loyal customer Loyal customers deserve special rewards and benefits.
linger v. 逗留；徘徊 linger on The smell of coffee lingered in the room all morning.
lighten v. 减轻；使明亮 lighten the mood A good joke can lighten the mood in any meeting.
landscape n. 风景；格局 competitive landscape The competitive landscape has changed dramatically this year.
longing n./adj. 渴望；思念的 a longing for She had a deep longing for her hometown during the holidays.
laughter n. 笑声 burst into laughter The audience burst into laughter at his witty remark.
laid-back adj. 悠闲的；随和的 laid-back attitude He has a laid-back attitude that puts everyone at ease.

技术相关

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配 例句
latency n. 延迟；等待时间 low latency We need low latency for real-time video streaming.
lint v./n. 代码检查 run lint Run lint before pushing your code to the repository.
library n. 库；代码库 import a library This library simplifies HTTP request handling.
load v./n. 加载；负载 load balancing Load balancing distributes traffic across multiple servers.
log n./v. 日志；记录 error log Check the error log to diagnose the issue.
loop n./v. 循环 for loop / infinite loop Be careful not to create an infinite loop in your code.
localhost n. 本地主机 run on localhost The dev server runs on localhost port 3000.
linter n. 代码检查工具 configure the linter We configured the linter to enforce consistent code style.
lambda n. 匿名函数；Lambda 服务 lambda expression Use a lambda expression to simplify the callback logic.

补充词汇

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配 例句
legacy n./adj. 遗留的；传统的 legacy system Migrating from the legacy system will take six months.
legitimate adj. 合法的；正当的 legitimate concern That is a legitimate concern we need to address.
lengthy adj. 冗长的；漫长的 lengthy process The approval process can be quite lengthy.
lucid adj. 清晰的；明了的 lucid explanation His lucid explanation helped the team understand the architecture.
lag n./v. 滞后；落后 time lag / lag behind There is a noticeable lag between input and screen response.
literate adj. 有文化的；精通的 digitally literate Being digitally literate is essential in the modern workplace.

常用短语

短语 含义 例句
look into 调查；研究 I'll look into the performance issue and report back tomorrow.
lay off 裁员；解雇 The company had to lay off 200 employees due to budget cuts.
level up 提升；升级 Taking this course will help you level up your coding skills.
let go of 放弃；释放 Sometimes you need to let go of legacy code and start fresh.
line up 安排；排列 We have three candidate interviews lined up for this afternoon.
lay the groundwork 打基础 This sprint lays the groundwork for the new payment system.
learn the ropes 学习入门知识 It took me a month to learn the ropes of the new framework.

学习提示

  • leverage 在商务英语中极为常见，既可作动词（利用），也可作名词（杠杆效应）。
  • latency 和 load balancing 是后端开发和系统设计面试中的核心词汇。
  • legacy 在技术语境中特指"遗留系统"，是代码重构和技术债务讨论中的高频词。
  • lint 是前端开发日常工作流的一部分，ESLint 是最流行的 JavaScript 代码检查工具。
  • look into 和 level up 是职场邮件和 Slack 消息中的高频短语。
  • lambda 在函数式编程和 AWS Lambda 服务两个语境中都非常重要。
  • laid-back 是描述性格或工作氛围的常用口语词，面试中描述团队文化时可以使用。
  • logistics 不仅用于物流行业，在项目管理中也常指活动的后勤安排。
  • liaison 是职场中描述跨部门协调角色的正式用词。
