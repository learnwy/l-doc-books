词典 E

职场与商务

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配 例句
efficiency n. 效率 improve/increase efficiency Automating tests improved our team's efficiency.
escalate v. 升级、上报 escalate an issue If the bug is not fixed by EOD, escalate to the manager.
estimate v./n. 估算 rough estimate, estimate the cost Can you give me a rough estimate of the timeline?
evaluate v. 评估 evaluate performance/options We need to evaluate all available solutions first.
execute v. 执行 execute a plan/strategy The team executed the rollout plan flawlessly.
expenditure n. 支出 capital expenditure Total expenditure exceeded the annual budget by 15%.
expertise n. 专业知识 technical expertise Her expertise in machine learning is invaluable.
eligible adj. 有资格的 eligible for promotion Employees with two years of service are eligible for the bonus.
equity n. 股权、公平 equity compensation, pay equity Early employees often receive equity as part of compensation.
endorse v. 认可、支持 endorse a proposal The VP endorsed the initiative to adopt TypeScript company-wide.
exempt adj./v. 免除的 tax-exempt, exempt from Nonprofits are exempt from certain tax obligations.
enterprise n. 企业 enterprise software We offer an enterprise plan with advanced security features.

日常与社交

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配 例句
embarrass v. 使尴尬 feel embarrassed I was embarrassed when I forgot her name.
empathy n. 共情、同理心 show empathy Showing empathy helps build stronger relationships.
enthusiastic adj. 热情的 enthusiastic about She is enthusiastic about learning new languages.
essential adj. 必要的 essential skills/items Sunscreen is essential for outdoor activities.
etiquette n. 礼仪 business etiquette Understanding local etiquette is important when traveling abroad.
enormous adj. 巨大的 enormous amount/pressure Moving to a new country requires an enormous effort.
exaggerate v. 夸大 exaggerate the problem He tends to exaggerate how busy he is at work.
encounter v./n. 遭遇 encounter a problem We encountered an unexpected issue during the trip.
eloquent adj. 雄辩的、有说服力的 eloquent speaker She gave an eloquent speech at the company anniversary.
errand n. 差事 run errands I need to run a few errands before going to the office.

技术相关

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配 例句
endpoint n. 端点 API endpoint The new endpoint returns paginated user data.
encryption n. 加密 end-to-end encryption All messages are protected with end-to-end encryption.
environment n. 环境 dev/staging/production environment Make sure to test in the staging environment first.
exception n. 异常 throw/catch an exception The function throws an exception when input is invalid.
extension n. 扩展、插件 browser extension, file extension Install this VS Code extension for better linting.
extract v. 提取 extract data/method We extracted the shared logic into a utility function.
emit v. 发出、触发 emit an event/signal The component emits a custom event when clicked.
enum n. 枚举 define an enum We use an enum to represent order status values.
ETL n. 数据抽取转换加载 ETL pipeline The ETL pipeline processes millions of records nightly.
edge case n. 边界情况 handle edge cases Always write tests for common edge cases.
event loop n. 事件循环 Node.js event loop Understanding the event loop is key to writing efficient Node.js code.

易混淆词辨析

词组 区别说明
economic vs. economical economic「经济的（与经济相关）」，economical「节省的」。Economic growth. / An economical solution.
emigrate vs. immigrate emigrate「移出」，immigrate「移入」。He emigrated from China. / She immigrated to Canada.
everyday vs. every day everyday 形容词「日常的」，every day 副词短语「每天」。Everyday tasks. / I exercise every day.
elicit vs. illicit elicit「引出」，illicit「非法的」。Elicit a response. / Illicit activity.

高频短语

短语 含义 例句
end up 最终 We ended up rewriting the entire module from scratch.
every other 每隔一个 We hold retrospectives every other week.
eat into 侵蚀、消耗 Technical debt is eating into our development speed.
early on 在早期 We identified the risk early on and mitigated it.
even though 尽管 Even though the deadline was tight, we delivered on time.
embark on 着手、开始 The team embarked on a major infrastructure overhaul.
end-to-end 端到端 We need end-to-end testing before the release.
err on the side of 宁可偏向 When in doubt, err on the side of caution.
ease into 逐步适应 It helps to ease into a new role during the first month.
evolve into 演变为 The prototype evolved into a full-featured product.
