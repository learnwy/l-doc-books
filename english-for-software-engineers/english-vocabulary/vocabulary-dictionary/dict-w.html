词典 W

职场与商务

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配 例句
wholesale n./adj. 批发；批发的 wholesale price, wholesale market We buy office supplies at wholesale prices.
workforce n. 劳动力，员工队伍 skilled workforce, remote workforce The company is investing in its workforce.
workflow n. 工作流程 approval workflow, workflow automation The new workflow reduced processing time by 40%.
warranty n. 保修，质保 warranty period, under warranty The laptop is still under warranty.
waive v. 放弃（权利），免除 waive the fee, waive a requirement The bank agreed to waive the late payment fee.
withdraw v. 撤回，提取 withdraw a proposal, withdraw funds She decided to withdraw her application.
worthwhile adj. 值得的 worthwhile investment, worthwhile effort The training program is a worthwhile investment.
workload n. 工作量 heavy workload, manage workload The team's workload has doubled since the merger.
wrap up phr.v. 完成，结束 wrap up a meeting, wrap up a project Let's wrap up this discussion and move to action items.
win-win adj. 双赢的 win-win situation, win-win solution We found a win-win solution that benefits both parties.
well-rounded adj. 全面的 well-rounded candidate, well-rounded education We're looking for a well-rounded candidate.

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配 例句

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配 例句

易混淆辨析

词组 区别说明
withdraw vs. retreat withdraw 偏正式（撤回申请、提取资金），retreat 偏指物理或军事撤退
worthwhile vs. worthy worthwhile 指值得花时间精力，worthy 指值得尊敬或配得上
widespread vs. universal widespread 指分布广泛但不一定全覆盖，universal 指全面的、普遍适用的
workflow vs. process workflow 侧重步骤和流转顺序，process 更泛指任何过程
wary vs. cautious wary 含有不信任的警觉感，cautious 只表示小心谨慎
witty vs. humorous witty 强调机智巧妙，humorous 更广泛地指幽默有趣
