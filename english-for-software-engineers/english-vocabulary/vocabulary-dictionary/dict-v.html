词典 V

职场与商务

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配 例句
vendor n. 供应商，卖方 third-party vendor, vendor management We signed a contract with a new cloud vendor.
viable adj. 可行的 viable option, viable solution Is this approach viable given our budget?
validate v. 验证，使生效 validate the data, validate the approach We need to validate the results before publishing.
venture n./v. 风险投资；冒险 joint venture, venture capital The startup attracted significant venture capital.
volatile adj. 不稳定的，易变的 volatile market, volatile situation The stock market has been highly volatile this week.
vacancy n. 空缺，职位空缺 job vacancy, fill a vacancy There's a vacancy in the marketing department.
volume n. 量，体积；音量 sales volume, high volume Order volume increased during the holiday season.
vest v. 授予（权力）；归属 vested interest, stock vest Her equity will vest over four years.
verify v. 核实，验证 verify identity, verify the claim Please verify your email address to continue.
vision n. 愿景，远见 company vision, strategic vision The founder shared her vision for the next decade.
value proposition n. 价值主张 core value proposition, strong value proposition Our value proposition focuses on speed and reliability.

日常与社交

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配 例句
versatile adj. 多才多艺的，多功能的 versatile tool, versatile person He's a versatile musician who plays five instruments.
vague adj. 模糊的，含糊的 vague answer, vague memory Her directions were too vague to follow.
voluntarily adv. 自愿地 voluntarily resign, voluntarily participate She voluntarily joined the cleanup effort.
vivid adj. 生动的，鲜明的 vivid description, vivid memory He gave a vivid account of his travels.
vulnerable adj. 脆弱的，易受伤害的 feel vulnerable, vulnerable population Children are especially vulnerable to misinformation.
vibrant adj. 充满活力的，鲜艳的 vibrant city, vibrant colors The vibrant street market was full of energy.
venture v. 敢于去，冒险尝试 venture out, venture into Few tourists venture into the remote mountain villages.
virtue n. 美德；优点 by virtue of, virtue of patience Patience is a virtue worth cultivating.
vanish v. 消失 vanish into thin air, vanish without a trace The cat vanished as soon as the door opened.
vigor n. 活力，精力 full of vigor, youthful vigor She attacked the problem with renewed vigor.

技术相关

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配 例句
variable n./adj. 变量；可变的 global variable, environment variable Store the API key in an environment variable.
version n. 版本 version control, latest version We released version 3.0 last week.
virtual adj. 虚拟的 virtual machine, virtual environment Run the tests inside a virtual machine.
viewport n. 视口 viewport width, viewport meta tag Set the viewport for responsive design.
verbose adj. 冗长的，详细的 verbose mode, verbose logging Enable verbose logging to debug the issue.
vulnerability n. 漏洞 security vulnerability, patch a vulnerability The security team discovered a critical vulnerability.
VPN n. 虚拟专用网络 connect to VPN, VPN tunnel Use the VPN to access internal resources remotely.
vector n. 向量；载体 attack vector, vector graphics SVG uses vector graphics for scalable images.
VM n. 虚拟机 spin up a VM, VM snapshot Spin up a VM to test the deployment in isolation.
v-dom n. 虚拟 DOM virtual DOM diffing, v-dom reconciliation React uses a virtual DOM to optimize rendering performance.

易混淆辨析

词组 区别说明
validate vs. verify validate 侧重确认是否符合规则，verify 侧重核实真实性
viable vs. feasible viable 强调可存活、可持续，feasible 强调技术上可行
vivid vs. vibrant vivid 强调清晰生动（常用于描述或记忆），vibrant 强调充满活力和能量
volatile vs. unstable volatile 常用于金融或技术领域，unstable 更通用
vague vs. ambiguous vague 指表达不够清晰，ambiguous 指有多种解读可能
vendor vs. supplier vendor 偏指零售或软件服务商，supplier 偏指原材料或批量供应商

常用短语与搭配

短语 含义 例句
value for money 物有所值 This laptop offers great value for money.
voice concern 表达担忧 Several team members voiced concerns about the timeline.
venture out 外出冒险 We ventured out despite the heavy rain.
vice versa 反之亦然 The frontend depends on the backend, and vice versa.
vow to 发誓，承诺 The CEO vowed to improve workplace safety.
viable alternative 可行的替代方案 Is there a viable alternative to this approach?
virtual presence 虚拟出席 Remote employees maintain a virtual presence via video calls.
value-added 增值的 This value-added service is included at no extra cost.
vet (a candidate) 审查（候选人） We need to vet all candidates before the final interview.
vis-a-vis 相对于，关于 Our position vis-a-vis the competition is strong.
vested interest 既得利益 He has a vested interest in the project's success.
voice of the customer 客户之声 Gather voice of the customer data before redesigning.
value stream 价值流 Map the value stream to identify bottlenecks.
volatility 波动性 Market volatility makes forecasting difficult.
vet thoroughly 彻底审查 Make sure to vet the library thoroughly before adopting it.
very well 非常好，完全地 This approach works very well for small teams.
vital role 至关重要的角色 Testing plays a vital role in software quality.
