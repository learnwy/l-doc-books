启动会与规划会议

项目启动

词汇 词性 含义 常用搭配
kickoff n. 启动会 project kickoff, kickoff meeting
charter n. 章程 project charter, team charter
objective n. 目标 project objective, clear objectives
deliverable n. 交付物 key deliverable, expected deliverables
timeline n. 时间表 project timeline, timeline review
RACI n. 责任分配矩阵 RACI matrix, define RACI

听说例句

A: Let's start the kickoff by reviewing the project charter. B: Sure. The objectives are defined, and I've prepared a RACI matrix so everyone knows their role.

A: What are the key deliverables for phase one? B: The API design doc, a working prototype, and the test plan. The timeline is six weeks.

写作表达

  • The project kickoff meeting established clear objectives, deliverables, and a timeline agreed upon by all stakeholders.
  • The RACI matrix ensures accountability by defining who is Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, and Informed for each task.

规划讨论

词汇 词性 含义 常用搭配
roadmap n. 路线图 product roadmap, quarterly roadmap
milestone n. 里程碑 key milestone, milestone tracking
resource n. 资源 allocate resources, resource planning
budget n. 预算 project budget, budget approval
stakeholder n. 利益相关者 key stakeholder, stakeholder input
risk assessment n. 风险评估 conduct risk assessment, risk matrix

听说例句

A: The roadmap has three major milestones for this quarter. B: Do we have the resources to hit all three? We should also do a risk assessment.

A: Has the budget been approved? B: Not yet. I need stakeholder input on the resource allocation before I can finalize.

写作表达

  • The quarterly roadmap defines three milestones with clear deliverables and resource requirements for each phase.
  • A thorough risk assessment during the planning phase helps identify potential blockers and allocate contingency resources.

OKR 与目标

词汇 词性 含义 常用搭配
OKR (Objectives and Key Results) n. 目标与关键结果 set OKRs, OKR review
objective n. 目标 quarterly objective, ambitious objective
key result n. 关键结果 measurable key result, achieve KR
initiative n. 举措 strategic initiative, launch an initiative
north star n. 北极星指标 align on north star, north star metric
cascade v. 层层分解 cascade goals, cascade from company OKRs

听说例句

A: Have you set your team OKRs for Q2? B: Yes. Our objective is to improve retention. The key results are: increase Day-7 retention by 10% and reduce churn by 5%.

A: How do team OKRs connect to the company's north star? B: They cascade from the company objective. Each initiative supports a specific key result.

写作表达

  • OKRs cascade from company-level objectives to team and individual initiatives, ensuring strategic alignment.
  • Each key result must be measurable and time-bound to enable objective evaluation at the end of the quarter.

会议管理

词汇 词性 含义 常用搭配
agenda n. 议程 set the agenda, follow the agenda
facilitator n. 主持人 meeting facilitator, assign a facilitator
note-taker n. 记录员 assign a note-taker, meeting notes
parking lot n. 停车场(暂存话题) add to parking lot, parking lot items
time check n. 时间提醒 do a time check, we're over time
next steps n. 下一步 define next steps, clear next steps

听说例句

A: Before we start, let me set the agenda. We have three items to cover in 30 minutes. B: I'll be the note-taker. If anything goes off-topic, let's add it to the parking lot.

A: Time check — we have 5 minutes left. B: Let's wrap up with next steps and action items.

写作表达

  • Every planning meeting should have a pre-defined agenda, a designated facilitator, and a note-taker.
  • Parking lot items are revisited in follow-up sessions to ensure that no important topics are permanently deferred.
