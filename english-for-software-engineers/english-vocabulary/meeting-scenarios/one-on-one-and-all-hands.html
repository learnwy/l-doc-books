一对一与全员会议

一对一 (One-on-One)

词汇 词性 含义 常用搭配
one-on-one n. 一对一会议 weekly one-on-one, manager 1:1
career growth n. 职业成长 discuss career growth, growth plan
feedback n. 反馈 give feedback, constructive feedback
goal setting n. 目标设定 quarterly goals, set goals
check-in n. 签到/碰头 quick check-in, weekly check-in
well-being n. 身心健康 team well-being, check on well-being

听说例句

A: In our one-on-one, I'd like to discuss my career growth plan. B: Absolutely. Let's review your quarterly goals and identify areas where you'd like more support.

A: How's everything going? Any concerns? B: Work is going well, but I've been feeling a bit burned out. I appreciate you checking on my well-being.

写作表达

  • Regular one-on-one meetings provide a dedicated space for career growth discussions and constructive feedback.
  • Checking on team members' well-being during 1:1s demonstrates empathetic leadership and builds trust.

绩效评估

词汇 词性 含义 常用搭配
performance review n. 绩效评估 annual review, mid-year review
self-assessment n. 自我评估 write self-assessment, self-assessment form
peer review n. 同事评审 360 peer review, peer feedback
strength n. 优势 key strengths, leverage strengths
area for improvement n. 改进领域 identify areas, improvement plan
rating n. 评级 performance rating, rating criteria

听说例句

A: It's time for the mid-year performance review. Have you completed your self-assessment? B: Yes, I've highlighted my key strengths and two areas for improvement.

A: The peer review feedback is very positive. B: That's great to hear. Let's discuss how I can leverage my strengths for the next project.

写作表达

  • The performance review process combines self-assessment, peer feedback, and manager evaluation for a holistic view.
  • Identifying both strengths and areas for improvement creates a balanced foundation for professional development.

全员会议 (All-Hands)

词汇 词性 含义 常用搭配
all-hands n. 全员会议 quarterly all-hands, company all-hands
town hall n. 公开会议 town hall meeting, town hall Q&A
announcement n. 公告 major announcement, company announcement
vision n. 愿景 company vision, share the vision
transparency n. 透明度 promote transparency, transparent communication
Q&A n. 问答 open Q&A, live Q&A session

听说例句

A: The CEO will share the company vision at the quarterly all-hands. B: Will there be an open Q&A session? I have questions about the new org structure.

A: There's a major announcement at the town hall today. B: I hope they promote transparency about the upcoming changes. People appreciate honest communication.

写作表达

  • Quarterly all-hands meetings provide leadership with an opportunity to share the company vision and celebrate team achievements.
  • Including an open Q&A session at town halls promotes transparency and gives employees a voice in organizational decisions.

团队建设

词汇 词性 含义 常用搭配
team building n. 团队建设 team building activity, team building event
icebreaker n. 破冰活动 icebreaker question, fun icebreaker
morale n. 士气 boost morale, team morale
recognition n. 认可 peer recognition, recognize contributions
celebration n. 庆祝 milestone celebration, team celebration
bonding n. 团队联结 team bonding, bonding activity

听说例句

A: Team morale has been low after the crunch period. Should we plan a team building event? B: Yes, a celebration for hitting the milestone would help. We could also add an icebreaker to the next all-hands.

A: I'd like to give peer recognition to Sarah for her outstanding work. B: Let's do it in the team meeting. Public recognition really boosts morale.

写作表达

  • Team building activities and milestone celebrations play an important role in maintaining high morale during demanding periods.
  • A culture of peer recognition reinforces positive behaviors and strengthens team bonding across the organization.
