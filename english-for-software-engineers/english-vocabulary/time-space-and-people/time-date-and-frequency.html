时间日期与频率顺序

概述

时间表达是英语沟通中不可或缺的一部分。无论是约定会议时间、描述工作进度还是叙述事件经过，都需要准确使用时间、日期、频率和顺序词汇。本文系统整理相关高频词汇和表达。

时间点与时段 (Time Points & Periods)

词汇 词性 含义 搭配
dawn n. 黎明 at dawn, from dawn to dusk
dusk n. 黄昏 at dusk
noon n. 正午 at noon, around noon
midnight n. 午夜 at midnight, past midnight
quarter n. 一刻钟 a quarter past/to
sharp adj./adv. 整点 at 3 o'clock sharp
approximately adv. 大约 approximately 2 hours
duration n. 持续时间 duration of the meeting
span n. 跨度 a time span of three months
deadline n. 截止日期 meet/miss the deadline

常用时间表达

表达 含义 示例
in the meantime 与此同时 In the meantime, let's prepare.
for the time being 暂时 For the time being, this solution works.
around the clock 全天候 working around the clock
in no time 很快 I'll finish it in no time.
from time to time 偶尔 I check in from time to time.
at the last minute 在最后一刻 changed plans at the last minute
ahead of time 提前 arrived ahead of time
behind schedule 落后于计划 The project is behind schedule.
on time 准时 The delivery arrived on time.
time-consuming 耗时的 a time-consuming process

听说例句

A: What time is the meeting tomorrow?
B: It's at 10 o'clock sharp. Please don't be late.
A: Got it. How long will it last?
B: The duration is approximately one hour.
A: Are we behind schedule on the project?
B: A little, but if we work around the clock this week,
   we can still meet the deadline.

日期与日历 (Dates & Calendar)

词汇 词性 含义 搭配
annual adj. 年度的 annual review, annual report
quarterly adj./adv. 季度的 quarterly meeting, report quarterly
monthly adj./adv. 每月的 monthly salary, meet monthly
biweekly adj./adv. 每两周一次 biweekly sync, meet biweekly
weekday n. 工作日 on weekdays
fiscal year n. 财年 fiscal year 2026
calendar year n. 自然年 within the calendar year
upcoming adj. 即将来临的 upcoming event, upcoming release
overdue adj. 逾期的 overdue payment, overdue task
reschedule v. 重新安排 reschedule the meeting

听说例句

A: When is the annual performance review?
B: It's scheduled for the first week of January.
A: Can we reschedule the biweekly sync? I have a conflict.
B: Sure. How about next Monday? The quarterly report is also
   due soon — it's already overdue by two days.
A: I'll get it done by end of the upcoming weekday.

频率与周期 (Frequency & Regularity)

词汇 词性 含义 搭配
frequently adv. 频繁地 frequently asked questions
occasionally adv. 偶尔 occasionally visit
rarely adv. 很少 rarely happen
constantly adv. 不断地 constantly changing
periodically adv. 周期性地 review periodically
intermittently adv. 间歇性地 work intermittently
consistently adv. 一贯地 consistently deliver
routinely adv. 常规地 routinely check
sporadic adj. 偶发的 sporadic outages
recurring adj. 反复出现的 recurring issue

频率从高到低

always (100%) → usually (~90%) → frequently (~80%)
→ often (~70%) → sometimes (~50%) → occasionally (~30%)
→ rarely (~10%) → seldom (~5%) → never (0%)

听说例句

A: How often do you deploy to production?
B: We deploy frequently — usually twice a week.
A: Any recurring issues?
B: We occasionally see sporadic timeouts, but they're
   consistently resolved within minutes.
A: Do you periodically review the deployment process?
B: Yes, we routinely audit it every quarter.

顺序与步骤 (Sequence & Order)

词汇 词性 含义 搭配
initially adv. 最初 initially planned
subsequently adv. 随后 subsequently discovered
simultaneously adv. 同时 run simultaneously
eventually adv. 最终 eventually resolved
prior to prep. 在…之前 prior to the launch
following prep. 在…之后 following the meeting
thereafter adv. 此后 and thereafter
meanwhile adv. 与此同时 Meanwhile, the team...
preceding adj. 前面的 the preceding chapter
consecutive adj. 连续的 three consecutive days

顺序信号词

位置 信号词
开始 First, To begin with, Initially, First of all
中间 Then, Next, After that, Subsequently, Meanwhile
结束 Finally, Lastly, In the end, Eventually, To conclude

听说例句

A: Can you walk me through the release process?
B: Sure. First, we run the automated tests. Then, the code
   goes through a peer review. Subsequently, it's deployed
   to staging for QA. Meanwhile, the docs team updates the
   changelog. Finally, we push to production.
A: What happens if tests fail?
B: The process stops. We fix the issue and start over.
   Eventually, all checks must pass before release.

写作表达

  • Prior to the system migration, a comprehensive backup was performed.
  • The team initially planned a two-week sprint but subsequently extended it to three.
  • Following the incident, the company conducted consecutive audits over three days.
  • The server experienced intermittent failures, which were eventually traced to a memory leak.
