地点方向与环境

概述

描述地点、指引方向和谈论环境是日常交流和职场沟通中的基础能力。无论是问路、描述办公环境还是讨论城市规划，都需要丰富的空间词汇。

地点与位置 (Places & Locations)

词汇 词性 含义 搭配
venue n. 场地、地点 meeting venue, event venue
premises n. 经营场所 on the premises, business premises
facility n. 设施 sports facility, public facilities
landmark n. 地标 famous landmark, near the landmark
suburb n. 郊区 in the suburbs, suburban area
downtown n./adv. 市中心 go downtown, downtown area
outskirts n. 郊区边缘 on the outskirts of the city
vicinity n. 附近 in the vicinity of, nearby vicinity
headquarters n. 总部 company headquarters, HQ
branch n. 分部 branch office, local branch

听说例句

A: Where is the venue for tomorrow's conference?
B: It's at the convention center downtown, near the park landmark.
A: Is there parking on the premises?
B: Yes, but it fills up fast. There are also public facilities
   in the vicinity — a parking garage two blocks away.
A: Is the branch office in the suburbs?
B: No, our headquarters and the branch are both downtown.

方向与导航 (Directions & Navigation)

词汇 词性 含义 搭配
intersection n. 交叉路口 at the intersection
roundabout n. 环形交叉 take the roundabout
shortcut n. 捷径 take a shortcut
straight ahead adv. 一直往前 Go straight ahead.
adjacent adj. 相邻的 adjacent to the building
opposite prep./adj. 对面的 opposite the station
parallel adj. 平行的 parallel to the road
alongside prep. 沿着 walk alongside the river
bypass n./v. 绕行/旁路 bypass the traffic
accessible adj. 可到达的 easily accessible by subway

指路常用句型

场景 表达
直走 Go straight ahead for two blocks.
转弯 Turn left/right at the intersection.
经过 Walk past the library, then...
在…旁边 It's right next to / adjacent to the bank.
对面 It's opposite the subway station.
到达 You'll see it on your left/right.
距离 It's about a 10-minute walk from here.

听说例句

A: Excuse me, how do I get to the nearest pharmacy?
B: Go straight ahead for about 200 meters. At the intersection,
   turn right. The pharmacy is adjacent to the coffee shop.
A: Is there a shortcut?
B: You can cut through the park — it's alongside the river.
   The pharmacy will be opposite the park exit.
A: Is it accessible by bus?
B: Yes, Bus 42 stops right there.

室内空间 (Indoor Spaces)

词汇 词性 含义 搭配
lobby n. 大堂 hotel lobby, office lobby
corridor n. 走廊 along the corridor
cubicle n. 隔间 office cubicle
conference room n. 会议室 book a conference room
pantry n. 茶水间 office pantry
storage n. 储物间 storage room, cloud storage
basement n. 地下室 in the basement
elevator n. 电梯 take the elevator to
staircase n. 楼梯 use the staircase
reception n. 前台 at the reception desk

听说例句

A: Where's the meeting?
B: Conference room 3A — third floor. Take the elevator and
   turn left along the corridor. It's the last door.
A: Where can I get water?
B: The pantry is next to the staircase on each floor.
A: And the reception?
B: It's in the lobby on the ground floor.

环境与自然 (Environment & Nature)

词汇 词性 含义 搭配
urban adj. 城市的 urban area, urban planning
rural adj. 乡村的 rural community
landscape n. 景观 natural landscape
vegetation n. 植被 dense vegetation
terrain n. 地形 rough terrain, flat terrain
climate n. 气候 tropical climate
pollution n. 污染 air/water pollution
sustainable adj. 可持续的 sustainable development
ecosystem n. 生态系统 marine ecosystem
conservation n. 保护 wildlife conservation

听说例句

A: What's the environment like in your city?
B: It's a major urban area, so air pollution can be an issue.
   But the city has invested heavily in sustainable development.
A: Are there green spaces?
B: Yes, there's a large park with diverse vegetation and a
   lake. The local government focuses on conservation efforts.
A: How about the surrounding area?
B: The outskirts are more rural, with beautiful natural landscapes
   and flat terrain perfect for cycling.

写作表达

  • The office is conveniently located in the vicinity of the subway station, making it easily accessible for employees.
  • The new campus is adjacent to a park, providing a pleasant work environment.
  • Urban expansion has led to the loss of natural ecosystems, highlighting the need for sustainable development.
