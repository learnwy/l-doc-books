求职信

核心概念

求职信（cover letter）是简历的补充文档，目的是将你的经历与特定职位建立直接关联。虽然并非所有职位都要求求职信，但一封精心撰写的求职信能显著提升你的面试几率——它展示了你对公司的研究、你的沟通能力以及你为什么是这个职位的理想人选。

核心原则是 It's about them, not you — 求职信的核心不是你有多优秀，而是你能为这个团队解决什么问题。


求职信的四段式结构
英文求职信遵循经典的四段式结构：Hook → Match → Evidence → Close。


第一段：Hook（开场钩子）


开场必须抓住注意力。避免平庸的开头如 I am writing to apply for the position of...。


好的开场方式：




When I read about your team's work on the next-generation recommendation engine at TechCorp, I immediately recognized the technical challenges — they're remarkably similar to the real-time personalization system I built at my current company, serving 5M+ daily active users.






Your recent blog post on migrating from a monolith to event-driven microservices resonated deeply with me. I led a similar transformation at DataFlow, and I'd love to bring that experience to your platform engineering team.






As a longtime user of your developer tools, I've seen firsthand how your API design philosophy simplifies complex workflows. I'm excited about the opportunity to contribute to this mission as a Senior Backend Engineer.




三种有效的开场策略：


    

  • 技术共鸣 — 将你的经验与公司的技术挑战联系起来
    • 

  • 产品认同 — 作为用户表达对产品的真实理解
    • 

  • 人脉推荐 — 提及推荐人：Sarah Chen on your team suggested I reach out...
    • 



第二段：Match（能力匹配）


将 JD 中的核心要求与你的经历直接对应：




Your team is looking for someone to lead the migration to Kubernetes and improve deployment reliability. In my current role, I architected a Kubernetes-based deployment platform that reduced deployment failures by 75% and cut rollback time from 30 minutes to under 2 minutes. I also introduced GitOps workflows using ArgoCD, which your job description mentions as a preferred skill.




第三段：Evidence（深度证据）


选择一个最有说服力的项目，展开讲述你的技术决策和影响：




One project I'm particularly proud of is the real-time fraud detection system I designed at FinTech Inc. When I joined, the existing system had a 15-minute detection lag, causing significant financial losses. I proposed and implemented a streaming architecture using Kafka and Flink that brought detection latency down to under 500ms. This directly prevented $2M in fraudulent transactions in its first quarter. The key technical challenge was maintaining consistency across distributed state while meeting strict latency SLAs — a problem I understand your team is also facing.




第四段：Close（结尾号召）


表达热情并推动下一步行动：




I'd welcome the opportunity to discuss how my experience with distributed systems and real-time processing could benefit your team. I'm available for a conversation at your convenience and can be reached at [email] or [phone]. Thank you for considering my application.




定制化策略与研究方法
每封求职信都必须针对具体公司和职位定制。一封通用求职信 = 没有求职信。


定制化的三个层次：


Level 3 的定制化需要深入研究：


    

  • 阅读公司的技术博客和工程文化文章
    • 

  • 查看团队成员在 GitHub、Twitter 上的技术分享
    • 

  • 了解公司最近的产品发布和技术挑战
    • 

  • 查看 Glassdoor 了解团队文化和面试流程
    • 



将研究成果自然融入求职信：




I noticed from your engineering blog that your team recently adopted a trunk-based development model. At my current company, I was the champion of this transition, and I documented the playbook that reduced our merge conflicts by 60%.

避免的常见错误
求职信中最常见的错误往往是看似无害却致命的：


过度谦虚：




I believe I might be a reasonable candidate for this position.




应改为：




I'm confident that my experience in distributed systems makes me a strong fit for this role.




空洞的自我评价：




I am a passionate, detail-oriented team player with excellent communication skills.




应改为具体的证据：




I led a cross-functional team of 8 engineers to deliver the payment platform 2 weeks ahead of schedule, a project that required close coordination with product, design, and compliance teams.




复述简历：


求职信不应重复简历中的内容。简历说 what，求职信解释 why 和 how。

远程与异地求职的特殊考量
当申请远程职位或异地公司时，求职信需要额外回应两个潜在疑虑：为什么选择远程 和 时区协作能力。


回应远程工作的能力：




I've worked remotely for the past 3 years and have developed strong async communication habits. I proactively document decisions, write detailed PR descriptions, and maintain a regular cadence of written status updates to keep my team aligned across time zones.




回应时区差异：




While I'm based in Shanghai (CST), I have significant overlap with PST working hours (my 9pm-1am aligns with your 6am-10am). I'm comfortable with a flexible schedule and have successfully collaborated with US-based teams in this arrangement for 2+ years.




回应"为什么选择我们公司"（尤其是异地申请时更需要说明）：




I'm specifically drawn to your team because of your commitment to distributed-first engineering culture, as described in your recent blog post "How We Build Across 5 Time Zones." This aligns perfectly with my own working style and values.




如果你计划搬迁到公司所在地：




I'm planning to relocate to the San Francisco Bay Area in Q3 2024 and would be happy to discuss timeline and logistics.




语气与格式的最终调整
求职信完成后，需要做最后的语气和格式调整。


语气校准——通读一遍，检查是否在自信和谦逊之间取得了正确的平衡：


    

  • 删除所有 I believe I might be... 类的犹豫表达
    • 

  • 确保至少有一处明确的自信陈述：I'm confident that... 或 My experience in X makes me well-suited for...
    • 

  • 避免在结尾使用 I hope to hear from you（太被动），改用 I'd welcome the opportunity to discuss...
    • 



格式标准：


    

  • 使用与简历相同的字体和边距，保持视觉统一
    • 

  • 收件人写法：Dear [Hiring Manager name], 或 Dear [Team] Hiring Team,
    • 

  • 如果不知道收件人姓名：Dear Hiring Manager,（不要用 To Whom It May Concern，太过时了）
    • 

  • 日期格式：March 15, 2024
    • 

  • 签名：Sincerely, 或 Best regards, + 全名
    •

小结
    

  • 求职信使用 Hook → Match → Evidence → Close 四段式结构
    • 

  • 开场避免 I am writing to apply for...，使用技术共鸣或产品认同切入
    • 

  • 核心原则是 It's about them, not you — 聚焦你能解决他们的什么问题
    • 

  • 第二段直接将 JD 要求与你的经历一一对应
    • 

  • 第三段选择最有说服力的项目展开讲述技术决策和量化影响
    • 

  • 每封求职信必须定制化，至少达到 Level 2（匹配 JD 关键词）
    • 

  • 避免空洞形容词、过度谦虚和复述简历
    • 

  • 控制在 3-4 段、半页以内
    •
