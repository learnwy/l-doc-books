案例研究写作

核心概念

案例研究（case study）是技术写作中最具说服力的形式。与教程教人"怎么做"不同，案例研究回答"为什么这样做"和"效果如何"。它通过真实的项目经历展示技术决策的过程、权衡和结果，是建立技术可信度和影响力的有力工具。

核心原则是 Show the journey, not just the destination — 展示决策过程的曲折和权衡，而不只是最终的光鲜结果。


案例研究的 SCARS 结构
技术案例研究推荐使用 SCARS 结构：Situation（背景）、Challenge（挑战）、Approach（方法）、Results（结果）、Summary/Lessons（总结/教训）。


Situation（背景）— 设定场景，让读者理解你的技术环境和业务约束：




At TechCorp, our e-commerce platform serves 5M daily active users across 12 countries. The order processing system, originally built as a monolith in 2018, was handling 50K orders per day. By Q3 2024, we were approaching 200K daily orders and the system was showing signs of strain — order processing latency had crept from 200ms to 2 seconds during peak hours.




好的背景描述包含：公司/团队规模、技术栈、业务指标、时间线。这些具体数字让案例研究更可信。


Challenge（挑战）— 明确定义问题，包括技术约束和业务约束：




The core challenge was threefold:


    

  1. Performance: p99 latency of 2 seconds was causing cart abandonment rates to spike by 15%
    2. 

  2. Reliability: The monolith had 3 production incidents in Q2, each causing 30+ minutes of downtime
    3. 

  3. Velocity: Deployment frequency had dropped from daily to weekly as the codebase grew to 500K lines
    4. 



The business constraint was equally challenging: we couldn't afford more than 2 hours of cumulative downtime during the migration, and Black Friday was 4 months away.




方法论与决策过程的展示
Approach 部分是案例研究的核心——读者最想看的不是你最终选了什么，而是你如何在多个选项之间权衡。


展示 decision matrix（决策矩阵）是最有效的方式：

We chose the hybrid approach for two reasons: it provided the risk mitigation of strangler fig while meeting our Black Friday deadline through parallel running of old and new systems.




展示被否决的方案及其原因同样重要：




Why not big-bang rewrite? While tempting for its speed, our team had previously attempted a big-bang migration in 2021 that failed after 3 months. The risk of repeating that failure, especially with Black Friday looming, was unacceptable.


Why not pure strangler fig? The 12-month timeline meant we wouldn't complete the migration before Black Friday. This was a business non-starter.




展示实施过程中遇到的意外问题和应对策略：




Three weeks into the migration, we discovered that the order processing logic had implicit dependencies on the monolith's database transaction boundaries. Our microservice design assumed eventually-consistent messaging, but certain order states required strong consistency.


How we adapted: We introduced a saga pattern with compensation transactions for the critical order flow, while keeping eventually-consistent messaging for non-critical paths like notification and analytics.




结果量化与诚实报告
Results 部分必须用数据说话。但同样重要的是诚实报告——包括预期之外的问题和未达成的目标。


好的结果报告格式：




Results (6 months post-migration)
Performance:


    

  • Order processing latency: 2s → 150ms (92% improvement)
    • 

  • p99 latency during Black Friday peak: 800ms (vs previous year's 5s+ timeouts)
    • 

  • Cart abandonment rate: decreased by 12%
    • 



Reliability:


    

  • Production incidents: 3 per quarter → 0 in the first quarter post-migration
    • 

  • Mean time to recovery (MTTR): 35 min → 5 min
    • 



Velocity:


    

  • Deployment frequency: weekly → daily (with the order service deploying 3x daily)
    • 

  • Lead time for changes: 2 weeks → 2 days
    • 



What didn't go as planned:


    

  • Infrastructure costs increased by 40% due to the overhead of running multiple services
    • 

  • The team needed 2 months to develop operational expertise with Kubernetes
    • 

  • One data consistency bug slipped through and affected 0.1% of orders in the first week
    • 





What didn't go as planned 部分是区分优秀案例研究和自吹自擂的关键。读者知道没有完美的项目，诚实地报告问题反而增加了整篇文章的可信度。


教训提炼与框架输出
案例研究的最终价值在于 transferable lessons（可迁移的教训）——读者能从你的经验中提取出适用于自己场景的原则。


教训的提炼模式——从具体到抽象：




Lessons Learned
1. Start with the data flow, not the service boundaries


Our initial service decomposition was based on organizational structure (order team, payment team, shipping team). This led to excessive cross-service communication. When we refactored based on data flow patterns, communication overhead dropped by 60%.


Generalizable principle: When decomposing a monolith, analyze data access patterns before drawing service boundaries. Conway's Law is real, but it shouldn't be your primary decomposition driver.


2. Invest in observability before you need it


We set up distributed tracing (Jaeger) and centralized logging (ELK) in week 1 of the migration, before writing any application code. This paid dividends every single week — we caught 4 production issues in staging that would have been nearly impossible to debug without end-to-end traces.


Generalizable principle: In any distributed system migration, observability is not a nice-to-have — it's a prerequisite.


3. The "parallel run" technique is underused


Running both old and new systems simultaneously and comparing outputs was the single most effective risk mitigation technique we employed. We found 23 discrepancies during the parallel run period that would have been production bugs.


Generalizable principle: For high-risk migrations, parallel running with output comparison is worth the infrastructure cost.




每个教训都遵循 Specific experience → Generalizable principle 的模式，让读者能直接将原则应用到自己的项目中。

案例研究的传播要素：


在文章中包含一张高质量的架构图（hero diagram），这是最常被截图分享的内容。确保图表在没有正文上下文时仍能传达核心信息。


撰写一个 150 词以内的 executive summary，可直接用于 LinkedIn 或 Twitter 分享：




We migrated our 5M-user e-commerce platform from monolith to microservices in 8 months, achieving 92% latency reduction, zero-downtime deployment, and 3x faster release cycles. Key lesson: start with data flow analysis, not org chart decomposition. Full case study: [link]




小结
    

  • 案例研究使用 SCARS 结构：Situation、Challenge、Approach、Results、Summary
    • 

  • 背景描述包含具体的业务数字（用户量、订单量、团队规模）
    • 

  • 展示决策矩阵和被否决方案，核心价值在于决策过程而非结论
    • 

  • 诚实报告 What didn't go as planned，增加文章可信度
    • 

  • 教训从具体经验提炼为可迁移的 generalizable principle
    • 

  • 核心原则是 Show the journey, not just the destination
    • 

  • 实施过程中的意外问题和应对策略是最有教育价值的部分
    •
