转述动词

核心概念

转述动词（Reporting Verbs）是用于引述他人言语、想法或建议的动词。最基础的转述动词是 say 和 tell，但英语中有丰富多样的转述动词。不同的转述动词不仅表达不同的语义（陈述、建议、承诺、警告等），还要求不同的语法结构。

转述动词是连接直接引语和间接引语的桥梁。使用丰富的转述动词可以让写作更加精确和生动，避免反复使用 said。在学术写作中，恰当使用转述动词还能表达你对被引用内容的态度（支持、质疑、中立）。

中文的转述动词（"说""告诉""建议"等）在语法搭配上比较自由。英语的转述动词则有严格的语法模式要求。每个转述动词都有固定的后续结构，不能随意替换。

示例





V + that 从句
say, claim, suggest
He said that he was tired.




V + O + that 从句
tell, inform, convince
She told me that she was leaving.




V + to do
agree, offer, promise
He agreed to help.




V + O + to do
ask, tell, advise
She asked me to wait.




V + doing
suggest, recommend, deny
He suggested going out.




V + O + prep + doing
accuse, blame, thank
She accused him of lying.





陈述类转述动词
陈述类转述动词用于转述他人的声明、断言和报告。


say 是最基础的转述动词，后面跟 that 从句。 He said that he was busy.。 say 不能直接跟间接宾语：He said me that... 是错误的。应该用：He said to me that he was busy. 或改用 tell。


tell 后面必须跟间接宾语（人），再跟 that 从句。 She told me that she would be late.。 Tell me what happened.。 He told us that the meeting was cancelled.。 tell 不能没有宾语：He told that... 是错误的。


claim 表示"声称"，暗示说话者可能不完全相信。 He claimed that he was innocent.（他声称自己是无辜的——暗示可能不是）。 The company claims that its product is the best.。


state 表示"正式陈述"，用于正式语境。 The minister stated that the policy would not change.。 The report states that sales increased by 20%.。


mention 表示"提到"，信息是顺带说的。 She mentioned that she was moving to a new city.。


explain 表示"解释"。 He explained that the delay was due to technical issues.。 explain 后面不跟间接宾语：He explained me that... 是错误的。应该是：He explained to me that...。


admit 表示"承认"，暗示不情愿或不利信息。 She admitted that she had made a mistake.。 He admitted to having been there.（admit to doing）。


deny 表示"否认"。 He denied that he had stolen the money.。 She denied knowing anything about it.（deny doing）。


announce 表示"宣布"。 The company announced that it would lay off 500 workers.。


建议与请求类转述动词
建议和请求类转述动词要求特定的语法结构，且许多涉及虚拟语气。


suggest 后面跟 doing 或 that + 虚拟语气。 She suggested going to the cinema.（suggest doing）。 She suggested that we go to the cinema.（虚拟语气）。 suggest 不能用 to do：She suggested to go... 是错误的。 suggest 也不能跟宾语 + 不定式：She suggested me to go... 是错误的。


recommend 的用法与 suggest 相同。 I recommend reading this book.（recommend doing）。 I recommend that you read this book.（虚拟语气）。 recommend 也不能用 recommend me to do 结构。


advise 的用法更灵活。 She advised me to see a doctor.（advise + O + to do）。 She advised seeing a doctor.（advise + doing）。 She advised that I see a doctor.（虚拟语气）。


ask 和 request 表示"请求"。 He asked me to help him.（ask + O + to do）。 She requested that all employees attend the meeting.（虚拟语气）。


urge 表示"催促、敦促"。 The doctor urged him to quit smoking.（urge + O + to do）。 They urged that action be taken immediately.（虚拟语气）。


insist 表示"坚持要求"。 He insisted that she attend the meeting.（虚拟语气）。 She insisted on paying for dinner.（insist on doing）。注意 insist 表"坚持认为"时不用虚拟语气：He insisted that he was innocent.。

承诺与警告类转述动词
promise 表示"承诺"。 He promised to finish the work by Friday.（promise + to do）。 She promised that she would help.（promise + that）。 He promised me that he would be on time.（promise + O + that）。


offer 表示"提出/主动提议"。 She offered to drive me to the airport.（offer + to do）。


agree 表示"同意"。 He agreed to attend the meeting.（agree + to do）。 They agreed that the proposal was reasonable.（agree + that）。


refuse 表示"拒绝"。 She refused to comment on the matter.（refuse + to do）。


threaten 表示"威胁"。 He threatened to call the police.（threaten + to do）。 She threatened that she would resign.（threaten + that）。


warn 表示"警告"。 She warned me not to go there alone.（warn + O + (not) to do）。 He warned that the road was dangerous.（warn + that）。 They warned us against investing in that company.（warn + O + against doing）。


remind 表示"提醒"。 She reminded me to lock the door.（remind + O + to do）。 He reminded us that the deadline was approaching.（remind + O + that）。


encourage 表示"鼓励"。 The teacher encouraged students to participate.（encourage + O + to do）。


persuade 表示"说服"。 I persuaded him to change his mind.（persuade + O + to do）。注意：persuade 暗示成功了。如果没有成功，用 try to persuade。


学术写作中的转述动词
学术写作中，转述动词的选择反映了作者对被引用内容的态度。不同的动词传达不同程度的支持、中立或质疑。


中立/客观：state、note、report、observe、describe。 Smith (2020) states that climate change is accelerating.。 The study notes that further research is needed.。这些动词不暗示任何态度，仅客观报告。


倾向支持：demonstrate、show、establish、confirm、reveal。 The data demonstrate a clear correlation.。 Previous research has confirmed this finding.。这些动词暗示被引用的内容有充分证据支持。


倾向质疑：claim、allege、assume、speculate。 Some researchers claim that the effect is minimal.。 The author assumes that all participants were honest.。这些动词暗示作者对被引用内容持保留或质疑态度。


强调论证：argue、contend、maintain、assert。 Johnson (2019) argues that traditional methods are inadequate.。 The authors maintain that their approach is superior.。这些动词暗示被引用的内容是一种论点或立场。


建议方向：suggest、propose、recommend、advocate。 The study suggests that early intervention is effective.。 They propose a new theoretical framework.。


在学术写作中，suggest 是最常用的转述动词之一。它的语气适中，既不过于断定也不过于质疑。


小结
    

  • say 后面不能直接跟间接宾语，tell 后面必须跟间接宾语
    • 

  • suggest 和 recommend 后面不能用 to do，也不能用 O + to do
    • 

  • explain 后面不能直接跟间接宾语：用 explain to me that
    • 

  • advise 是最灵活的建议动词，支持 doing、O + to do 和虚拟语气
    • 

  • insist 表"坚持要求"用虚拟语气，表"坚持认为"用陈述语气
    • 

  • persuade 暗示说服成功，未成功用 try to persuade
    • 

  • 学术转述动词的选择反映作者对引用内容的态度
    • 

  • 中立动词（state、note）适用于客观报告
    • 

  • claim 和 assume 暗示质疑态度，使用时要注意语义色彩
    • 

  • 丰富的转述动词能让写作更精确，避免反复使用 said
