议论文写作

概述

议论文（Argumentative Essay）是英语写作中最重要的文体之一，广泛用于学术写作、标准化考试（雅思、托福、GRE）和职业沟通。议论文的核心是提出明确的论点（Thesis），并用逻辑严密的论证和可靠的证据来说服读者。

议论文的基本结构

五段式结构（Five-Paragraph Essay）

部分 段数 内容
Introduction 1 段 引入话题 → 背景 → 论点（Thesis Statement）
Body 3 段 每段一个分论点 + 证据 + 分析
Conclusion 1 段 重述论点 → 总结 → 展望/呼吁

进阶结构

对于更复杂的议论文，可以在 Body 中加入反方论点的反驳：

Introduction
  ↓
Body 1: 分论点 1 + 证据 + 分析
  ↓
Body 2: 分论点 2 + 证据 + 分析
  ↓
Body 3: 分论点 3 + 证据 + 分析
  ↓
Body 4: 反方观点 + 反驳（Counter-argument + Rebuttal）
  ↓
Conclusion

论点（Thesis Statement）

什么是好的论点

论点是全文的核心主张，通常在引言段末尾。

特征 好的论点 差的论点
有争议性 Online education should replace traditional classrooms for university courses. Online education exists.
具体明确 Social media causes anxiety in teenagers by promoting unrealistic body standards. Social media is bad.
可论证 Governments should invest more in renewable energy to combat climate change. Climate change is happening.
范围适中 Public libraries should offer free coding workshops to reduce the digital divide. Education needs to change.

论点的两种类型

直接陈述型：

Mandatory community service in high schools builds empathy,
develops practical skills, and strengthens community bonds.

让步 + 转折型：

Although social media connects people globally, its negative
impact on mental health outweighs its communication benefits.

引言段写作

引言段的漏斗结构

宽泛的背景介绍（General Background）
    ↓  逐渐缩小范围
具体的问题或现象（Specific Issue）
    ↓
论点（Thesis Statement）

引言段开头技巧

技巧 示例
引人注目的事实 Every year, 8 million tons of plastic enter the ocean...
引用名言 As Einstein once said, "Imagination is more important than knowledge."
提出问题 Should governments have the right to monitor citizens' online activity?
场景描述 Imagine a world where every student has access to personalized AI tutoring.
对比/矛盾 While technology connects us globally, many feel more isolated than ever.

引言段示例

In recent decades, the global education system has undergone
a dramatic transformation with the rise of digital technology.
Online learning platforms such as Coursera, edX, and Khan Academy
now offer courses from world-renowned universities to anyone with
an internet connection. While some educators argue that online
education lacks the depth of traditional classroom instruction,
the evidence suggests otherwise. [论点] Online education should be
integrated into mainstream university curricula because it increases
accessibility, reduces costs, and allows for personalized learning
experiences.

主体段写作

PEEL 结构

每个主体段遵循 PEEL 结构：

字母 含义 作用
P Point（论点） 提出本段分论点
E Evidence（证据） 提供支撑证据
E Explanation（解释） 分析证据如何支撑论点
L Link（衔接） 回扣论点或过渡到下一段

证据类型

证据类型 可信度 示例
统计数据 高 A 2024 study found that 78% of remote workers...
专家观点 高 According to Dr. Smith, a leading psychologist...
研究结果 高 Research published in Nature indicates...
真实案例 中 In Singapore, the government implemented...
个人经历 低 In my experience as a teacher...
常识/逻辑 中 It stands to reason that...

主体段示例

[Point] First, online education significantly increases
accessibility for students worldwide. [Evidence] According to
UNESCO (2023), over 250 million children globally lack access
to quality education due to geographic or economic barriers.
Online platforms can reach these underserved populations at a
fraction of the cost of building physical schools. [Explanation]
This means that a student in a rural village in India can access
the same lecture from an MIT professor as a student in Boston,
effectively democratizing education. [Link] Therefore, integrating
online education into universities is not just a convenience but
a moral imperative for global educational equity.

反方论点与反驳

为什么要包含反方观点

  • 展示你全面思考了问题
  • 增强论证的说服力
  • 学术写作的基本要求

反驳策略

策略 描述 信号词
直接反驳 指出反方观点的错误 However, this argument fails because...
让步 + 转折 承认部分有理但论点更强 While it is true that..., however...
证据反驳 用数据推翻反方观点 Research actually shows the opposite...
范围限定 承认在某些情况下对但不普遍 Although this may apply to..., in most cases...

反驳段示例

[反方观点] Opponents of online education argue that it lacks
the social interaction essential for student development.
[让步] Admittedly, face-to-face interaction offers unique benefits
for building interpersonal skills. [反驳] However, modern online
platforms have evolved to include discussion forums, video
conferencing, and collaborative projects that effectively replicate
classroom interaction. [证据] A 2023 study by Stanford University
found that students in well-designed online courses reported similar
levels of peer engagement as those in traditional classrooms.
[结论] Thus, the social interaction argument against online
education is increasingly outdated.

结论段写作

结论段的结构

重述论点（用不同措辞）
    ↓
总结主要论据
    ↓
展望/呼吁行动/提出更广泛的意义

结论段注意事项

应该做 不应该做
用新的措辞重述论点 逐字重复引言段
总结核心论据 引入新的论据
提出行动建议或展望 以问题结尾（让读者困惑）
给读者留下深刻印象 用 "In my opinion" 弱化立场

议论文常用表达

表达观点

It is widely acknowledged that...
There is growing evidence that...
It can be argued that...
This essay contends that...

表示让步

While it is true that...
Admittedly, ...
It must be acknowledged that...
Despite the fact that...

表示强调

Indeed, ...
It is particularly noteworthy that...
What is especially significant is...
The evidence overwhelmingly suggests that...

实践建议

  • 写之前先列提纲：论点 + 3 个分论点 + 各自证据
  • 每段只围绕一个分论点展开
  • 先写主体段，再写引言和结论
  • 用反方论点展示批判性思维
  • 多读范文，积累学术表达
  • 写完后检查：每个分论点是否支持总论点？证据是否充分？
