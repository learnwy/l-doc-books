常见写作错误修正

概述

英语写作错误可以分为语法错误、词汇错误、句式错误和篇章错误四个层面。许多错误源自中文思维的直接迁移（即"中式英语"），另一些则是英语自身规则的陷阱。本文系统整理最常见的写作错误及其修正方法，帮助学习者写出更准确、更地道的英语。

中式英语（Chinglish）

词汇层面的中式英语

中文思维 中式英语 地道英语
打开电视 open the TV turn on the TV
关上灯 close the light turn off the light
学习知识 study knowledge acquire/gain knowledge
提高能力 improve ability develop/enhance skills
身体很好 My body is very good I am in good health
交通方便 The traffic is convenient The location is easily accessible
给你带来不方便 bring you inconvenience cause you inconvenience
价格便宜 The price is cheap The price is low / It is inexpensive

句式层面的中式英语

"因为…所以…" 双重连词

❌ Because it was raining, so we stayed home.
✅ Because it was raining, we stayed home.
✅ It was raining, so we stayed home.

"虽然…但是…" 双重连词

❌ Although he is old, but he is still active.
✅ Although he is old, he is still active.
✅ He is old, but he is still active.

主语缺失

中文允许省略主语，英语通常不行：

❌ If want to succeed, must work hard.
✅ If you want to succeed, you must work hard.

❌ Is very important to exercise.
✅ It is very important to exercise.

"很" 的过度使用

❌ I very like this book.
✅ I really like this book. / I like this book very much.

❌ She is very beautiful very much.
✅ She is very beautiful.

句子层面的常见错误

1. 逗号拼接（Comma Splice）

用逗号连接两个独立句子是英语写作的常见错误：

❌ The weather was cold, we stayed inside.

✅ 修正方法（四选一）：
  (a) 加连词：The weather was cold, so we stayed inside.
  (b) 用分号：The weather was cold; we stayed inside.
  (c) 分成两句：The weather was cold. We stayed inside.
  (d) 用从句：Because the weather was cold, we stayed inside.

2. 残句（Fragment）

缺少主语或谓语的不完整句子：

❌ Because I was tired. （从句不能独立成句）
✅ Because I was tired, I went to bed early.

❌ The student studying in the library.（缺少谓语动词）
✅ The student was studying in the library.

❌ For example, smartphones and laptops.（不是完整句子）
✅ For example, smartphones and laptops are widely used.

3. 串句（Run-on Sentence）

两个独立句子没有任何连接就合在一起：

❌ She loves reading she goes to the library every day.
✅ She loves reading, so she goes to the library every day.
✅ She loves reading; she goes to the library every day.

4. 悬垂修饰语（Dangling Modifier）

修饰语的逻辑主语与句子主语不一致：

❌ Walking to school, the rain started. （雨在走路？）
✅ Walking to school, I got caught in the rain.

❌ Having finished the homework, the TV was turned on.
✅ Having finished the homework, she turned on the TV.

5. 平行结构错误

并列的成分应保持相同的语法形式：

❌ She enjoys reading, swimming, and to play tennis.
✅ She enjoys reading, swimming, and playing tennis.

❌ The job requires patience, creativity, and being organized.
✅ The job requires patience, creativity, and organization.

❌ He is not only smart but also works hard.
✅ He is not only smart but also hardworking.

词汇层面的常见错误

易混淆词对

错误用法 正确用法 区别
❌ affect (n.) effect (n.) affect 是动词，effect 是名词
❌ principle (人) principal (人) principal = 校长/主要的
❌ complement (赞美) compliment (赞美) complement = 补充
❌ stationary (文具) stationery (文具) stationary = 静止的
❌ discrete (谨慎) discreet (谨慎) discrete = 离散的
❌ farther (更深入) further (更深入) farther = 物理距离更远

词性误用

❌ The develop of technology is rapid.
✅ The development of technology is rapid.

❌ She success in her career.
✅ She succeeded in her career.

❌ This is a importantly issue.
✅ This is an important issue.

搭配错误

❌ do a decision → ✅ make a decision
❌ strong rain → ✅ heavy rain
❌ open the computer → ✅ turn on the computer
❌ depend of → ✅ depend on
❌ married with → ✅ married to

篇章层面的常见问题

1. 缺乏主题句

每个段落应该有明确的主题句引领：

❌ （没有主题句，直接堆砌细节）
Many students use smartphones. They also use laptops.
Some students prefer tablets. Technology is everywhere.

✅ （有主题句，逻辑清晰）
Modern students rely heavily on digital devices for learning.
Many use smartphones to access educational apps, while others
prefer laptops for writing assignments. Tablets have also gained
popularity for their portability and touchscreen functionality.

2. 逻辑跳跃

论证缺少过渡，观点之间缺乏衔接：

❌ Exercise is important for health. The government should
build more parks.（逻辑跳跃）

✅ Exercise is important for maintaining physical and mental
health. However, many urban residents lack convenient access
to exercise facilities. Therefore, the government should invest
in building more parks and recreational spaces to encourage
physical activity.（逻辑完整）

3. 重复和冗余

❌ In my personal opinion, I think that...
   （personal + opinion + I think 三重冗余）
✅ In my opinion, ... 或 I believe that...

❌ The reason is because...（reason 和 because 语义重复）
✅ The reason is that... 或 This is because...

❌ return back / repeat again / advance forward
✅ return / repeat / advance

写作自查清单

语法检查

  • 主谓一致（单复数）
  • 时态一致（前后统一）
  • 冠词使用（a/an/the/零冠词）
  • 介词搭配正确
  • 无逗号拼接或残句

词汇检查

  • 无中式英语表达
  • 词性使用正确
  • 搭配地道
  • 无拼写错误
  • 无易混淆词误用

句式检查

  • 平行结构对称
  • 修饰语位置正确
  • 句子长短搭配
  • 无不必要的被动语态

篇章检查

  • 每段有主题句
  • 段落间有过渡词衔接
  • 论证逻辑清晰
  • 无重复冗余表达
  • 开头和结尾呼应

推荐校对工具

工具 功能 特点
Grammarly 语法、拼写、风格 最全面，有免费版
ProWritingAid 深度文体分析 适合长文写作
Hemingway Editor 句子复杂度分析 帮助简化表达
LanguageTool 多语言语法检查 开源免费

实践建议

  • 每次写完文章，对照自查清单逐项检查
  • 建立个人"错误日志"，记录反复犯的错误
  • 让母语者或工具帮你批改，关注反馈中的模式
  • 每周针对一种错误类型做专项练习
  • 多读优秀英文范文，培养正确的语感
