商务邮件与正式写作

概述

商务邮件（Business Email）和正式写作（Formal Writing）是职场和学术场景中最常用的英语写作形式。与日常交流不同，正式写作有严格的格式要求、用词规范和语气标准。掌握这些技能，能帮助你在国际化工作环境中高效、专业地沟通。

商务邮件的基本结构

邮件组成部分

部分 说明 示例
Subject Line 简洁明确的主题行 Re: Q3 Marketing Budget Approval
Greeting 称呼 Dear Mr. Johnson, / Hi Team,
Opening 开头（目的/背景） I am writing to inquire about...
Body 主体（核心内容） 详细内容，分段清晰
Closing 结尾（行动要求） Please let me know by Friday.
Sign-off 结束语 Best regards, / Sincerely,
Signature 签名 姓名、职位、联系方式

主题行写作原则

原则 好例子 差例子
简洁（5-8 词） Meeting Reschedule: Wed to Thu About the meeting that was supposed to happen
包含关键信息 Invoice #2024-0156 Payment Reminder Important!!
标注行动 Action Required: Approve Budget by 3/15 Budget
标注紧急程度 Urgent: Server Down - Need Immediate Fix Help!

什么场景用什么语气

场景 语气 称呼 结束语

上级/客户 中性/正式 Dear [Mr./Ms. 姓], Best regards,
首次联系陌生人 正式 Dear Mr./Ms. [姓], Sincerely,
投诉/索赔 正式 Dear Sir/Madam, Yours faithfully,

常见商务邮件场景

1. 请求（Request）

Subject: Request for Project Timeline Extension

Dear Ms. Chen,

I hope this email finds you well.

I am writing to request a two-week extension for the Alpha
project deliverables. Due to unexpected technical challenges
with the API integration, our team requires additional time
to ensure quality standards are met.

The revised timeline would move the deadline from April 15
to April 29. This adjustment will not affect the overall
project budget or scope.

I would appreciate your approval at your earliest convenience.
Please let me know if you would like to discuss this further.

Best regards,
David Wang
Senior Developer

2. 跟进（Follow-up）

Subject: Follow-up: Marketing Proposal Submitted on 3/20

Dear Mr. Johnson,

I am writing to follow up on the marketing proposal I submitted
on March 20. I wanted to ensure you received it and to see if
you have any questions or feedback.

I would be happy to schedule a brief call to walk you through
the key recommendations if that would be helpful.

Looking forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,
Sarah Li

3. 道歉（Apology）

Subject: Apology for Delayed Delivery - Order #A2024-789

Dear Ms. Thompson,

I sincerely apologize for the delay in delivering your order.
Due to an unexpected supply chain disruption, we were unable
to meet the original delivery date of March 25.

To rectify this situation, we have expedited your shipment,
which is now scheduled to arrive by March 28. Additionally,
we would like to offer a 15% discount on your next order as
a gesture of goodwill.

We value your business and will take steps to prevent such
delays in the future. Please do not hesitate to contact me
if you have any concerns.

Sincerely,
James Liu
Customer Service Manager

4. 通知（Announcement）

Subject: Office Closure - National Holiday (April 4-6)

Dear Team,

Please be advised that our office will be closed from
April 4 to April 6 for the national holiday.

Key reminders:
- All urgent requests should be submitted by April 3, 5:00 PM.
- Emergency contacts are listed on the shared drive.
- Normal operations will resume on April 7.

Please plan your tasks accordingly. If you have any questions,
feel free to reach out.

Best regards,
HR Department

正式写作核心原则

1. 用词正式化

非正式 正式替代
get obtain, acquire, receive
give provide, offer, supply
help assist, facilitate, support
start commence, initiate, begin
need require, necessitate
think believe, consider, maintain
show demonstrate, illustrate, indicate
big significant, substantial, considerable
good effective, beneficial, favorable
bad adverse, detrimental, unfavorable

2. 避免非正式表达

避免 替代
a lot of a significant number of / considerable
kind of / sort of somewhat / to some extent
stuff / things items / matters / elements
get rid of eliminate / remove
find out determine / ascertain
缩写（don't, can't） do not, cannot
俚语（cool, awesome） excellent, outstanding

3. 使用名词化表达

名词化（Nominalization）使表达更正式、紧凑：

动词/形容词 名词化
We analyzed the data. The analysis of the data...
The economy grew rapidly. The rapid growth of the economy...
They failed to comply. Their failure to comply...
It is important to plan. The importance of planning...

4. 使用被动语态

正式写作中被动语态比主动语态更常见：

主动：We conducted the experiment in January.
被动：The experiment was conducted in January.

主动：The committee approved the proposal.
被动：The proposal was approved by the committee.

邮件礼仪要点

规则 说明
24 小时内回复 即使无法完整回复，先确认收到
控制长度 商务邮件理想长度：5-10 句
一封一主题 不要在一封邮件中讨论多个话题
谨慎使用 Reply All 确认所有收件人确实需要看到回复
检查附件 提到附件时确认已添加
校对再发 检查拼写、语法和收件人
避免全大写 ALL CAPS 被视为喊叫

常用正式写作短语库

开头

I am writing to inform you that...
I am writing in response to your email dated...
Further to our conversation on [date]...
With reference to your inquiry about...
I would like to bring to your attention...

正文

Please find attached...
As discussed in our meeting...
I would like to draw your attention to...
It has come to my attention that...
Please be advised that...

结尾

Please do not hesitate to contact me if you have any questions.
I look forward to hearing from you.
I would appreciate your prompt response.
Thank you for your time and consideration.
Should you require further information, please let me know.

实践建议

  • 收集优秀的商务邮件范例，建立个人"邮件模板库"
  • 写邮件前先想清楚：目的是什么？读者需要做什么？
  • 每封邮件写完后至少检查一遍再发送
  • 用 Grammarly 或类似工具辅助校对
  • 模仿专业人士的邮件风格，逐步提升
