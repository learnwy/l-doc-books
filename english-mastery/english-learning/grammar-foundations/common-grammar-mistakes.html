常见语法错误

概述

语法错误是英语学习者最常遇到的障碍。许多错误源于母语（中文）思维的干扰，另一些则是英语自身不规则规则的陷阱。本文整理了中国英语学习者最高频的语法错误，帮助你在写作和口语中避免这些问题。

主谓一致错误

第三人称单数忘加 -s

❌ She go to school every day.
✅ She goes to school every day.

集合名词的单复数

名词 英式英语 美式英语
team The team are ready. The team is ready.
family My family are all here. My family is all here.

不可数名词误用复数

❌ I need some informations.
✅ I need some information.

❌ He gave me many advices.
✅ He gave me much advice.  /  He gave me several pieces of advice.

常见不可数名词：information, advice, furniture, equipment, luggage, homework, research

时态使用错误

现在完成时 vs. 一般过去时

❌ I have visited Paris last year.
✅ I visited Paris last year.

❌ I already finished my homework.
✅ I have already finished my homework.

规则：有具体过去时间标志（yesterday, last week, in 2020）→ 用一般过去时。

时间/条件从句中误用将来时

❌ I will call you when I will arrive.
✅ I will call you when I arrive.

❌ If it will rain, we will stay home.
✅ If it rains, we will stay home.

冠词使用错误

遗漏冠词

❌ I am student.
✅ I am a student.

❌ Sun rises in east.
✅ The sun rises in the east.

冠词多余

❌ I like the music.（泛指音乐）
✅ I like music.

❌ The life is beautiful.（泛指生活）
✅ Life is beautiful.

a vs. an

❌ a hour, a honest person
✅ an hour, an honest person（按发音而非字母）

❌ an university, an uniform
✅ a university, a uniform（/juː/ 是辅音音素）

介词使用错误

常见介词搭配

正确用法 常见错误
depend on ❌ depend of
consist of ❌ consist with
different from ❌ different with
married to ❌ married with
arrive at/in ❌ arrive to
listen to ❌ listen (无介词)

中式英语的介词干扰

❌ I very like it.（受中文"我很喜欢"影响）
✅ I like it very much.

❌ Although...but...（虽然…但是…）
✅ Although he is tired, he keeps working.（不能同时用 although 和 but）

代词与指代错误

代词指代不明

❌ Tom told Jerry that he was wrong.（he 指谁？）
✅ Tom told Jerry, "You are wrong."  或  Tom told Jerry that Jerry was wrong.

it vs. there 的混用

❌ It has a book on the table.
✅ There is a book on the table.

❌ There is raining.
✅ It is raining.

词性混淆

形容词 vs. 副词

❌ She sings beautiful.
✅ She sings beautifully.

❌ I feel badly about it.
✅ I feel bad about it.（feel 是系动词，后接形容词）

常见词性混淆对

名词 动词 形容词 副词
success succeed successful successfully
difference differ different differently
economy economize economic/economical economically

句子结构错误

悬垂修饰语（Dangling Modifier）

❌ Walking down the street, the trees were beautiful.
   （树在走路？）
✅ Walking down the street, I noticed the beautiful trees.

平行结构（Parallelism）

❌ She likes reading, to swim, and plays tennis.
✅ She likes reading, swimming, and playing tennis.

❌ The course is both interesting and a challenge.
✅ The course is both interesting and challenging.

逗号拼接（Comma Splice）

❌ It was raining, we stayed home.
✅ It was raining, so we stayed home.
✅ It was raining; we stayed home.
✅ Because it was raining, we stayed home.

拼写与标点陷阱

易混淆词

词 A 词 B 区别
its it's its（它的） vs. it's（it is / it has）
their there / they're 他们的 / 那里 / they are
affect effect 动词（影响） vs. 名词（效果）
than then 比较 vs. 然后
lose loose 丢失 vs. 松的

自查清单

写完英语文章后，按以下清单逐项检查：

  1. 每个句子是否有主语和谓语？
  2. 主谓是否一致（单复数）？
  3. 动词时态是否正确且前后一致？
  4. 冠词是否该加的加了、不该加的去掉了？
  5. 介词搭配是否正确？
  6. 代词指代是否清晰？
  7. 修饰语位置是否合理？
  8. 平行结构是否对称？
  9. 是否有逗号拼接？
  10. 易混淆词是否用对了？

实践建议

  • 把自己的写作中反复出现的错误记录在"错误本"上
  • 请母语者或语法检查工具（如 Grammarly）辅助校对
  • 每周集中练习一类语法点，逐个攻克
  • 朗读自己的文章，耳朵往往比眼睛更容易发现错误
