备忘录与通知

核心概念

备忘录（Memo / Memorandum）和通知（Notice / Announcement）是组织内部沟通的两种核心文档类型。备忘录用于在组织内部传达信息、提出建议或记录决策，而通知则用于向特定群体正式发布信息、政策变更或事件安排。

在英语商务环境中，备忘录和通知都有着明确的格式规范和语言风格。与邮件相比，它们更加正式和结构化，常用于需要存档、传阅或作为组织记录的场合。掌握这两种文档的写作规范，能帮助你在组织中高效、专业地传达重要信息。

备忘录的标准格式
英文备忘录有着非常固定的格式规范。标准的 memo 头部包含四个必要字段。


标准格式：




MEMORANDUM


TO: All Department Heads FROM: Sarah Chen, VP of Operations DATE: October 15, 2024 RE: Updated Expense Reimbursement Policy




[正文内容]




头部字段说明：


    

  • TO：收件人（可以是个人、团队或"All Staff"）
    • 

  • FROM：发件人（包含姓名和职位）
    • 

  • DATE：日期（使用完整格式：October 15, 2024）
    • 

  • RE 或 SUBJECT：主题（简洁明了，概括核心内容）
    • 



正文结构：


备忘录的正文通常遵循"目的→背景→详情→行动"的四段式结构。


第一段：说明目的


开门见山，用一句话说明这份备忘录为什么而写：


    

  • The purpose of this memo is to inform all employees of the updated expense reimbursement policy, effective November 1, 2024.
    • 

  • I am writing to propose changes to our current project review process.
    • 

  • This memo summarizes the key decisions made during the Q3 strategic planning session.
    • 



中间段落：提供背景和细节


按逻辑顺序展开关键信息，使用标题、编号列表和表格增强可读性：




Background: Over the past six months, we have seen a 40% increase in expense reimbursement processing time, primarily due to incomplete submissions and unclear guidelines. An internal audit identified three key areas for improvement.


Key Changes:


    

  1. All expense reports must be submitted within 10 business days of the expense date (previously 30 days)
    2. 

  2. Receipts are required for all expenses exceeding 25(previously25 (previously25(previously50)
    3. 

  3. Pre-approval is now required for all travel expenses exceeding $1,000
    4. 





结尾段落：明确行动和联系方式


    

  • Please review the attached policy document and direct any questions to the Finance team at finance@company.com.
    • 

  • I welcome your feedback on this proposal. Please share your thoughts by October 25 so we can finalize the plan.
    • 

  • If you have any concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to me directly.
    • 



通知的写作规范
通知比备忘录更简短、更直接，通常用于向大范围受众发布单一信息。


公司公告（Company Announcement）：




NOTICE


Date: October 15, 2024 To: All Employees From: Human Resources Department Subject: Office Closure — Thanksgiving Holiday


Please be advised that the office will be closed on Thursday, November 28, and Friday, November 29, 2024, in observance of the Thanksgiving holiday. Normal business hours will resume on Monday, December 2.


If you need to access the office during the closure, please contact Building Security at ext. 5555 for arrangements.

备忘录的高级写作场景
除了日常信息传达，备忘录在以下高级场景中也发挥着关键作用。


分析型备忘录（Analytical Memo）：


用于向决策者呈现分析结果和建议。结构通常包含：问题陈述 → 分析 → 选项评估 → 建议。




RE: Recommendation for New CRM Platform Selection


Purpose: This memo presents the evaluation results of three CRM platforms and recommends a course of action for the sales team.


Background: Our current CRM system (legacy Platform X) will reach end-of-life in March 2025. The evaluation committee assessed three alternatives based on functionality, cost, integration capability, and user experience.

决策记录型备忘录（Decision Memo）：


用于记录重要决策的背景、过程和结果，作为组织档案保留：




RE: Decision Record — Q4 Marketing Budget Allocation


Decision: The leadership team has approved a Q4 marketing budget of $500,000, allocated as follows: Digital Marketing (40%), Content Marketing (25%), Events (20%), Brand (15%).


Context: This represents a 15% increase over Q3, driven by the upcoming product launch in November and the seasonal increase in customer acquisition costs.


Key Considerations: The team discussed reducing event spending in favor of digital channels but decided to maintain the allocation given the confirmed sponsorship at TechConf 2024.


Approved by: CMO, CFO Date: October 15, 2024




语气与受众适配
备忘录和通知的语气需要根据内容和受众进行调整。


传达好消息：


    

  • We are delighted to announce...
    • 

  • I'm pleased to share that...
    • 

  • It is with great pleasure that we inform you...
    • 



传达中性信息：


    

  • Please be advised that...
    • 

  • This is to inform you that...
    • 

  • For your information, the following changes will take effect...
    • 



传达坏消息或限制性政策：


    

  • After careful consideration, we have made the difficult decision to...
    • 

  • Due to [reason], we regret to inform you that...
    • 

  • While we understand this may cause inconvenience, this change is necessary to ensure...
    •

小结
    

  • 备忘录和通知是组织内部正式沟通的核心文档，比邮件更结构化、更适合存档
    • 

  • 备忘录的标准格式包含 TO/FROM/DATE/RE 四个头部字段，正文遵循"目的→背景→详情→行动"结构
    • 

  • 通知比备忘录更简短直接，核心是"什么变了/发生了、什么时候、需要做什么"
    • 

  • 分析型备忘录是向决策者呈现选项和建议的有效工具，使用表格对比增强说服力
    • 

  • 语气应根据消息性质和受众调整——好消息用积极语气，坏消息用同理心加解释
    • 

  • 所有内部文档的结尾都应包含明确的行动号召和联系方式
    • 

  • 好的内部文档不仅传达信息，还帮助读者理解"为什么"和"接下来做什么"
    •
