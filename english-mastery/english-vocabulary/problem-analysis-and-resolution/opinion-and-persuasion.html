观点表达与说服

表达观点

词汇 词性 含义 常用搭配
opinion n. 意见 in my opinion, strong opinion
perspective n. 视角 from my perspective, different perspective
viewpoint n. 观点 share a viewpoint, opposing viewpoints
stance n. 立场 take a stance, firm stance
convinced adj. 确信的 fully convinced, I'm convinced that
believe v. 相信 I believe, firmly believe

听说例句

A: What's your perspective on the new pricing model? B: In my opinion, it's too aggressive. I believe we should test it with a small segment first.

A: The marketing team has a different viewpoint on this. B: I understand their stance, but from an engineering perspective, the timeline is not realistic.

写作表达

  • From a product perspective, we firmly believe that simplifying the onboarding flow will improve conversion.
  • While we respect differing viewpoints, we take the stance that data privacy must remain the top priority.

说服技巧

表达 含义 场景
The data shows that... 数据表明... 用事实说服
Based on our experience... 根据我们的经验... 借助经验
If we consider the long-term impact... 如果考虑长期影响... 引导思考
One key advantage is... 一个关键优势是... 强调好处
The risk of not doing this is... 不做的风险是... 指出风险
Let me walk you through the reasoning. 让我带你理清逻辑 系统论证

听说例句

A: Why should we invest in automated testing now? B: The data shows that teams with high test coverage ship 40% faster. The risk of not doing this is increasing tech debt.

A: I'm not sure we need to refactor this module. B: Let me walk you through the reasoning. If we consider the long-term impact, refactoring now saves us three months of maintenance.

写作表达

  • Based on our experience with similar migrations, we estimate a 25% reduction in operational costs.
  • One key advantage of the proposed architecture is its ability to scale horizontally without code changes.

同意与反对

词汇 词性 含义 常用搭配
agree v. 同意 agree with, agree on
disagree v. 不同意 respectfully disagree, disagree on
support v. 支持 support the proposal, fully support
oppose v. 反对 oppose the change, strongly oppose
acknowledge v. 承认 acknowledge the concern, acknowledge that
concede v. 让步 concede the point, willing to concede

听说例句

A: I support the proposal to adopt TypeScript for the new project. B: I respectfully disagree. I acknowledge the type safety benefits, but the migration cost is too high right now.

A: Can we at least agree on the timeline? B: I'm willing to concede on the scope if we keep the original deadline.

写作表达

  • While we acknowledge the concerns raised by the QA team, we support the proposed release schedule.
  • The committee unanimously agreed on the revised roadmap after extensive deliberation.

谈判与协商

词汇 词性 含义 常用搭配
negotiate v. 谈判 negotiate terms, negotiate a deal
leverage n./v. 筹码/利用 use as leverage, leverage strengths
mutual adj. 互相的 mutual benefit, mutual agreement
stakeholder n. 利益相关者 key stakeholder, stakeholder buy-in
buy-in n. 认同/支持 get buy-in, stakeholder buy-in
consensus n. 共识 reach consensus, build consensus

听说例句

A: We need stakeholder buy-in before moving forward. B: Let me set up a meeting to build consensus. I'll leverage the pilot results to make the case.

A: The vendor wants to renegotiate the contract terms. B: We should negotiate from a position of strength. Our usage volume gives us leverage.

写作表达

  • Building consensus across cross-functional teams requires transparent communication and mutual respect.
  • The negotiation resulted in a mutually beneficial agreement that addressed the concerns of all key stakeholders.
