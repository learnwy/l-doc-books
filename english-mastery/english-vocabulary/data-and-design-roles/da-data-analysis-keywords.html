DA 数据分析关键词

分析方法

词汇 词性 含义 常用搭配
cohort analysis n. 群组分析 run cohort analysis, cohort retention
funnel analysis n. 漏斗分析 conversion funnel, funnel drop-off
segmentation n. 分群 user segmentation, behavioral segmentation
trend analysis n. 趋势分析 perform trend analysis, identify trends
root cause analysis n. 根因分析 conduct RCA, find root cause
attribution n. 归因 attribution model, multi-touch attribution

听说例句

A: Can you run a cohort analysis on the new user group? B: Sure. I'll segment by signup week and track Day-7 and Day-30 retention for each cohort.

A: Where is the biggest drop-off in the funnel? B: The funnel analysis shows 40% drop-off between product page and cart. I need to do a root cause analysis.

写作表达

  • Cohort analysis revealed that users acquired through organic channels have 20% higher retention than paid users.
  • A multi-touch attribution model provides a more accurate view of channel contribution across the customer journey.

数据工具

词汇 词性 含义 常用搭配
SQL n. 结构化查询语言 write SQL queries, SQL optimization
dashboard n. 仪表盘 build a dashboard, analytics dashboard
visualization n. 可视化 data visualization, visualization tool
spreadsheet n. 电子表格 create a spreadsheet, spreadsheet analysis
BI (Business Intelligence) n. 商业智能 BI tool, BI platform
ETL n. 数据提取转换加载 ETL pipeline, ETL process

听说例句

A: Can you build a dashboard for the marketing team? B: Sure. I'll write the SQL queries first and connect them to our BI tool for real-time visualization.

A: How do you handle the data from different sources? B: We have an ETL pipeline that extracts, transforms, and loads data into our central warehouse.

写作表达

  • The analytics dashboard provides real-time visibility into key metrics, powered by optimized SQL queries.
  • The ETL pipeline processes over 10 million records daily, enabling timely and accurate business intelligence.

业务指标

词汇 词性 含义 常用搭配
KPI (Key Performance Indicator) n. 关键绩效指标 track KPIs, meet KPI targets
North Star metric n. 北极星指标 define North Star, align on North Star
ARPU (Average Revenue Per User) n. 每用户平均收入 increase ARPU, calculate ARPU
LTV (Lifetime Value) n. 用户终身价值 estimate LTV, LTV:CAC ratio
churn rate n. 流失率 reduce churn, monthly churn rate
NPS (Net Promoter Score) n. 净推荐值 measure NPS, improve NPS

听说例句

A: What's our North Star metric? B: Weekly active buyers. All other KPIs — ARPU, churn rate, NPS — ladder up to that.

A: How do we calculate LTV? B: ARPU multiplied by average customer lifespan. We should also factor in the churn rate.

写作表达

  • Aligning the organization around a single North Star metric ensures that all teams are driving toward the same goal.
  • Reducing monthly churn rate by 2% is projected to increase LTV by approximately $15 per user.

报告与沟通

词汇 词性 含义 常用搭配
insight n. 洞察 actionable insight, data-driven insight
recommendation n. 建议 provide recommendations, data-backed recommendation
narrative n. 叙事/故事线 data narrative, build a narrative
stakeholder n. 利益相关者 present to stakeholders, stakeholder meeting
executive summary n. 执行摘要 write executive summary, brief summary
deep dive n. 深入分析 do a deep dive, deep dive report

听说例句

A: Can you prepare an executive summary for the leadership meeting? B: Sure. I'll include the key insights, a data narrative, and three actionable recommendations.

A: Should we do a deep dive into the churn spike? B: Absolutely. I'll present the findings to the stakeholders next week.

写作表达

  • The executive summary distills complex data into three actionable insights for senior leadership.
  • A compelling data narrative helps stakeholders understand the "why" behind the numbers and supports informed decision-making.
