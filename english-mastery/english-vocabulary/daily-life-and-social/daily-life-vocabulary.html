日常生活词汇

概述

日常生活词汇是英语交流的基础。本章涵盖衣食住行、家居生活、购物消费、健康卫生等核心场景词汇，每个词提供词性、含义、搭配、听说例句和写作表达。

饮食 (Food & Drink)

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配
appetite n. 食欲、胃口 have a good/poor appetite, lose one's appetite
ingredient n. 食材、成分 key/main ingredient, fresh ingredients
recipe n. 食谱、做法 follow a recipe, share a recipe
cuisine n. 菜系、烹饪风格 local/Chinese/Italian cuisine
leftovers n. 剩饭剩菜 heat up leftovers, save leftovers
takeout n. 外卖 order takeout, get takeout
grocery n. 食品杂货 grocery store, buy groceries
beverage n. 饮料 hot/cold beverage, alcoholic beverage
nutrition n. 营养 good nutrition, nutrition facts
portion n. 分量、份 large/small portion, portion control

听说例句

A: What do you feel like eating tonight?
B: I don't have much appetite. Maybe just some soup?
A: How about I make that chicken soup recipe from last week?
B: Sounds good. Do we have all the ingredients?
A: Let me check. We might need to pick up some groceries.

写作表达

  • The restaurant is known for its authentic Sichuan cuisine.
  • Maintaining proper nutrition is essential for a healthy lifestyle.
  • She followed the recipe carefully and added all the ingredients in order.

居住 (Housing & Home)

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配
lease n./v. 租约/签租约 sign a lease, lease expires
tenant n. 租户 current tenant, find a tenant
landlord n. 房东 contact the landlord
utilities n. 水电煤气费 pay utilities, utility bills
maintenance n. 维修保养 building maintenance, maintenance fee
furnish v. 配备家具 fully furnished, furnish an apartment
renovate v. 翻新改造 renovate the kitchen, renovation project
commute n./v. 通勤 daily commute, commute to work
neighborhood n. 社区、邻里 quiet neighborhood, friendly neighborhood
mortgage n. 房贷 pay the mortgage, mortgage rate

听说例句

A: How's the new apartment?
B: It's great! The landlord just renovated the kitchen.
A: Nice. Is it fully furnished?
B: Mostly. I need to buy a desk. The commute is only 20 minutes.
A: What about utilities?
B: They're included in the lease, which is super convenient.

写作表达

  • The tenant signed a one-year lease for the apartment.
  • Monthly utilities including water, electricity, and internet cost about $150.
  • The neighborhood is safe and has excellent public transportation for commuting.

购物与消费 (Shopping & Spending)

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配
bargain n./v. 便宜货/讨价还价 get a bargain, bargain with sb.
discount n. 折扣 get/offer a discount, 20% discount
receipt n. 收据 keep the receipt, show your receipt
refund n./v. 退款 get/request a refund, full refund
exchange v./n. 换货 exchange for a different size
warranty n. 保修 under warranty, warranty period
budget n./v. 预算 on a budget, budget for sth.
expense n. 开支 daily expenses, living expenses
affordable adj. 负担得起的 affordable price, affordable housing
overcharge v. 多收费 be overcharged for sth.

听说例句

A: I got this jacket on sale — 40% discount!
B: What a bargain! Make sure you keep the receipt.
A: Of course. It has a 30-day return policy for a full refund.
B: Smart. I need to be more careful with my budget this month.
A: Same here. My living expenses have gone up recently.

写作表达

  • The product comes with a two-year warranty covering all manufacturing defects.
  • Consumers on a tight budget often look for affordable alternatives.
  • If you are overcharged, present your receipt to request a refund.

健康与卫生 (Health & Hygiene)

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配
symptom n. 症状 common symptoms, show symptoms
prescription n. 处方 write/fill a prescription
allergy n. 过敏 food allergy, allergic to sth.
immune adj. 免疫的 immune system, immune response
hygiene n. 卫生 personal hygiene, hygiene standards
pharmacy n. 药房 go to the pharmacy
appointment n. 预约 make/book an appointment
checkup n. 体检 annual checkup, routine checkup
recover v. 恢复 recover from an illness, full recovery
remedy n. 疗法、补救 home remedy, natural remedy

听说例句

A: I've been having headaches and a runny nose.
B: Those sound like allergy symptoms. Have you seen a doctor?
A: Not yet. I should make an appointment.
B: You could also try a home remedy first — honey and ginger tea.
A: Good idea. I'll pick up some from the pharmacy on my way home.

写作表达

  • Patients should describe their symptoms clearly when visiting the doctor.
  • Maintaining good personal hygiene helps strengthen the immune system.
  • After recovering from the flu, she went for a routine checkup.

交通出行 (Transportation)

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配
transfer v./n. 换乘/转车 transfer to Line 2, bus transfer
congestion n. 拥堵 traffic congestion, congestion charge
pedestrian n. 行人 pedestrian crossing, pedestrian zone
detour n./v. 绕道 take a detour, road detour
navigation n. 导航 GPS navigation, navigation app
fare n. 车费 bus/taxi fare, fare increase
intersection n. 十字路口 at the intersection, busy intersection
destination n. 目的地 reach the destination, final destination
departure n. 出发 departure time, departure gate
delay n./v. 延误 flight delay, delayed by 2 hours

听说例句

A: The traffic congestion is terrible today.
B: Let me check the navigation app for a detour.
A: Good idea. What time is our departure?
B: The flight is at 3 PM, but there might be a delay.
A: We should leave early. The fare to the airport is about $30.

写作表达

  • Due to heavy congestion, commuters are advised to take the subway and transfer at Central Station.
  • Pedestrians should use the designated crossing at each intersection.
  • The departure was delayed by one hour due to severe weather conditions.

天气与季节 (Weather & Seasons)

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配
forecast n./v. 天气预报/预测 weather forecast, forecast rain
humid adj. 潮湿的 hot and humid, humid climate
breeze n. 微风 gentle breeze, cool breeze
drizzle n./v. 毛毛雨 light drizzle
frost n. 霜冻 morning frost, frost warning
heatwave n. 热浪 summer heatwave
overcast adj. 阴天的 overcast sky
sunshine n. 阳光 bright sunshine, hours of sunshine
temperature n. 温度 high/low temperature, room temperature
precipitation n. 降水 average precipitation, precipitation level

听说例句

A: What does the weather forecast say for tomorrow?
B: It'll be overcast with a chance of drizzle in the morning.
A: At least the heatwave is over. It was so humid last week.
B: True. I heard there might be frost this weekend.
A: Time to bring out the winter coats!

写作表达

  • The forecast predicts a temperature drop of 10 degrees with possible frost overnight.
  • Despite the overcast sky, a gentle breeze made the afternoon pleasant.
  • Annual precipitation in this region averages 1,200 mm, with a humid summer season.
