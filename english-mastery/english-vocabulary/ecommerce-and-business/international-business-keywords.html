国际商务关键词

商务沟通

词汇 词性 含义 常用搭配
negotiate v. 谈判 negotiate a deal, negotiate terms
contract n. 合同 sign a contract, contract terms
partnership n. 合作关系 strategic partnership, build partnership
vendor n. 供应商 vendor management, preferred vendor
procurement n. 采购 procurement process, procurement team
quotation n. 报价 request a quotation, submit a quotation

听说例句

A: Have we signed the contract with the new vendor? B: Not yet. We're still negotiating the payment terms. I'll request an updated quotation by Friday.

A: Is the partnership with the logistics company confirmed? B: Yes, it's a strategic partnership covering procurement and last-mile delivery for three markets.

写作表达

  • The procurement team reviewed quotations from five vendors before selecting the preferred supplier.
  • A strategic partnership was established to strengthen our presence in the Southeast Asian market.

财务与预算

词汇 词性 含义 常用搭配
revenue n. 收入 annual revenue, revenue growth
profit n. 利润 net profit, profit margin
budget n. 预算 within budget, budget allocation
expense n. 费用 operating expense, reduce expenses
ROI n. 投资回报率 calculate ROI, positive ROI
forecast n./v. 预测 financial forecast, forecast revenue

听说例句

A: Are we on track to meet the revenue target? B: We're slightly below forecast, but the Q4 campaign should close the gap. The profit margin looks healthy.

A: Can we get more budget for the marketing team? B: We need to show positive ROI from the current spend first. Let me put together the numbers.

写作表达

  • Annual revenue exceeded the forecast by 12%, driven by strong performance in the Asia-Pacific region.
  • Budget allocation for Q3 has been adjusted to prioritize high-ROI marketing channels.

全球化战略

词汇 词性 含义 常用搭配
expansion n. 扩张 global expansion, market expansion
penetration n. 渗透 market penetration, penetration rate
diversification n. 多元化 product diversification, revenue diversification
scalability n. 可扩展性 business scalability, ensure scalability
competitive advantage n. 竞争优势 gain competitive advantage, maintain advantage
market share n. 市场份额 increase market share, dominant market share

听说例句

A: What's our market penetration rate in Latin America? B: About 3%. We see huge potential for expansion there, especially in Brazil and Mexico.

A: How do we maintain our competitive advantage? B: Through product diversification and scalability. We need to stay ahead of local competitors.

写作表达

  • The company's global expansion strategy targets five new markets, with a focus on increasing market share in each.
  • Revenue diversification across multiple regions reduces dependency on any single market.

商务礼仪

表达 含义 场景
It's a pleasure to meet you. 很高兴认识您 初次见面
Thank you for your time. 感谢您的时间 会议结束
We look forward to a long-term partnership. 期待长期合作 合作意向
Please feel free to reach out. 请随时联系我们 开放沟通
We appreciate your prompt response. 感谢您的及时回复 跟进感谢
Shall we schedule a follow-up call? 我们安排一次跟进电话？ 后续安排

听说例句

A: It's a pleasure to meet you, Mr. Chen. Thank you for making time for this meeting. B: Likewise. We look forward to a long-term partnership with your company.

A: We appreciate your prompt response on the pricing. B: Of course. Shall we schedule a follow-up call to discuss the contract details?

写作表达

  • We sincerely appreciate your time and look forward to exploring a mutually beneficial partnership.
  • Please feel free to reach out at any time should you require additional information or clarification.
