词典 J

本页收录以字母 J 开头的实用词汇，涵盖职场、日常和技术三大场景。

职场与商务

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配 例句
justify v. 证明…合理；为…辩护 justify the decision Can you justify the budget increase for Q3?
jeopardize v. 危害；使…处于危险 jeopardize the project Delays could jeopardize the entire launch timeline.
joint adj. 联合的；共同的 joint venture The two companies formed a joint venture in Asia.
jurisdiction n. 管辖权；管辖范围 under the jurisdiction of This matter falls under the jurisdiction of the legal team.
junior adj./n. 初级的；资历较浅的 junior developer She started as a junior analyst two years ago.
job description n. 职位描述 update the job description The job description clearly outlines required qualifications.
jet lag n. 时差反应 suffer from jet lag I always suffer from jet lag after long-haul business trips.
jobseeker n. 求职者 active jobseeker The platform connects jobseekers with top tech employers.

日常与社交

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配 例句
journey n. 旅程；历程 a long journey The journey from the airport takes about an hour.
joyful adj. 快乐的；高兴的 joyful moment It was a joyful reunion after five years apart.
judge v./n. 判断；法官 judge by appearance Don't judge a book by its cover.
juggle v. 同时应付；兼顾 juggle multiple tasks Parents often juggle work and family responsibilities.
jest n./v. 玩笑；开玩笑 in jest He said it in jest, but she took it seriously.
jealous adj. 嫉妒的；吃醋的 jealous of She was jealous of her colleague's promotion.
journal n. 日记；期刊 keep a journal I keep a journal to track my daily thoughts.
jam n./v. 拥堵；卡住 traffic jam I was stuck in a traffic jam for two hours.
janitor n. 管理员；看门人 building janitor The janitor keeps the office building clean every day.
jargon n. 行话；专业术语 industry jargon Try to avoid jargon when explaining things to clients.
jot v. 草草记下 jot down notes Let me jot down the key points from the meeting.
jovial adj. 愉快的；友好的 jovial personality His jovial personality makes him popular at social events.

技术相关

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配 例句
JSON n. JSON 数据格式 parse JSON The API returns data in JSON format.
JIT n. 即时编译 JIT compilation JIT compilation improves runtime performance significantly.
JVM n. Java 虚拟机 run on JVM Kotlin code can run on the JVM alongside Java.
job n. 作业；任务（技术语境） cron job The cron job runs every night at midnight.
join v./n. 连接；关联（数据库） inner join / left join Use a left join to include all records from the first table.
JWT n. JSON Web Token issue a JWT The server issues a JWT after successful authentication.
JSX n. JavaScript XML 语法扩展 write JSX React components use JSX to describe the UI structure.
JDK n. Java 开发工具包 install JDK Make sure you have JDK 17 or later installed on your machine.

补充词汇

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配 例句
jubilant adj. 欢欣鼓舞的 jubilant crowd The team was jubilant after winning the contract.
junction n. 交汇点；连接处 at the junction Turn left at the junction to reach the office.
jumble n./v. 混乱；混杂 a jumble of wires The server room was a jumble of cables and devices.
judicious adj. 明智的；审慎的 judicious use of Judicious use of resources is critical for startups.
juxtapose v. 并列；对比 juxtapose two options The report juxtaposes last year's data with current trends.
juncture n. 关键时刻；节点 at this juncture At this juncture, we need to decide on the tech stack.

常用短语

短语 含义 例句
jump on a call 快速加入电话会议 Can we jump on a call to discuss this issue?
just in time 恰好及时 The fix was deployed just in time before the demo.
jump the gun 操之过急 Let's not jump the gun before we see the test results.
join forces 联手；合力 The two departments joined forces to complete the migration.
jack of all trades 多面手；样样通 A full-stack developer is often a jack of all trades.
jump through hoops 历经周折 We had to jump through hoops to get the compliance approval.
just around the corner 即将来临 The product release is just around the corner.

学习提示

  • justify 在职场写作中高频出现，常用于报告和提案中解释决策依据。
  • juggle 是描述多任务处理的生动用词，面试中可用来描述自己的工作能力。
  • JSON / JWT / JVM 是技术面试和日常开发中的核心缩写词，务必熟记。
  • jargon 本身就是一个关于"术语"的术语，在跨部门沟通中尤为重要。
  • jump on a call 是远程办公中最常用的口语表达之一，比 "have a meeting" 更加随意自然。
  • juxtapose 是学术和商务报告中的高级词汇，表示将两个事物放在一起进行对比。
  • juncture 在正式场合中使用，表示"关键时刻"，比 moment 更加书面。
  • jobseeker 在招聘和人力资源领域中高频出现，注意英式写法为一个词。
