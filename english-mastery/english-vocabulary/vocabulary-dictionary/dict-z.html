词典 Z

职场与商务

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配 例句
zealous adj. 热情的，积极的 zealous supporter, zealous advocate She's a zealous advocate for workplace diversity.
zone n./v. 区域；划分区域 time zone, comfort zone, free-trade zone The meeting is at 3 PM in their time zone.
zero-sum adj. 零和的 zero-sum game, zero-sum thinking Negotiation doesn't have to be a zero-sum game.
zenith n. 顶点，鼎盛时期 reach the zenith, at its zenith The company reached its zenith in the early 2000s.
zero in on phr.v. 聚焦于，瞄准 zero in on the problem Let's zero in on the root cause of this issue.
zero-based adj. 从零开始的 zero-based budgeting Zero-based budgeting requires justifying every expense from scratch.

日常与社交

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配 例句
zeal n. 热情，热忱 with great zeal, religious zeal He approached every project with great zeal.
zigzag n./v./adj. 之字形；曲折前行 zigzag pattern, zigzag path The trail zigzags up the mountainside.
zip v. 拉上拉链；快速移动 zip up, zip through She zipped through the crowd to catch the bus.
zen n./adj. 禅；平静的 zen garden, zen moment I find a zen moment every morning with my tea.
zest n. 热情，兴致；（柑橘的）皮 zest for life, lemon zest Her zest for life is truly infectious.
zone out phr.v. 走神，发呆 zone out during class I tend to zone out when meetings run too long.
zilch n. 零，什么都没有 zilch, got zilch I searched for an hour and found zilch.
zippy adj. 快速的，敏捷的 zippy performance, zippy car The new electric scooter is surprisingly zippy.
zany adj. 滑稽的，古怪的 zany humor, zany character The movie's zany humor appealed to all age groups.

技术相关

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配 例句
zip v./n. 压缩；压缩文件 zip file, zip archive Zip the folder before uploading it to the server.
zero-day n./adj. 零日（漏洞） zero-day exploit, zero-day vulnerability A zero-day vulnerability was discovered in the framework.
z-index n. 层叠顺序（CSS） set z-index, z-index value Increase the z-index to bring the modal to the front.
zoom v./n. 缩放 zoom in, zoom out, zoom level Users can zoom in on the map for street-level detail.
zombie n. 僵尸进程 zombie process, zombie connection The server had several zombie processes consuming memory.
zsh n. Z Shell 命令行 zsh configuration, switch to zsh macOS uses zsh as the default shell since Catalina.
Zookeeper n. 分布式协调服务 Zookeeper cluster, Zookeeper node Kafka relies on Zookeeper for cluster coordination.
zlib n. 压缩库 zlib compression, zlib deflate The server uses zlib compression to reduce response size.

技术词汇速查表

Z 开头的英语词汇较少，但在技术领域有若干重要术语：

词汇 领域 说明
z-index CSS 控制元素在页面上的堆叠层级
zero-day 安全 指尚未被厂商修复的安全漏洞
zip/unzip 文件 文件压缩与解压缩
zombie process 操作系统 已结束但未被父进程回收的子进程
Zookeeper 分布式系统 Apache 开源的分布式协调框架
zsh 命令行 功能强大的 Unix Shell，macOS 默认 Shell
zlib 压缩 广泛使用的数据压缩库

易混淆辨析

词组 区别说明
zeal vs. enthusiasm zeal 带有更强烈的使命感和执着，enthusiasm 更偏日常的热情
zone vs. area zone 强调划定的功能区域（如时区、禁区），area 泛指地区
zip vs. compress zip 特指 ZIP 格式压缩，compress 泛指任何压缩方式
