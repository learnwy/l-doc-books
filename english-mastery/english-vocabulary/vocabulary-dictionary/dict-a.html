词典 A

职场与商务

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配 例句
accomplish v. 完成、达成 accomplish a goal/task We accomplished all sprint targets this quarter.
accountable adj. 负责的 hold sb. accountable Each team member is accountable for their deliverables.
agenda n. 议程 meeting agenda, set the agenda Please review the agenda before the standup.
allocate v. 分配 allocate resources/budget The manager allocated extra budget for Q3 marketing.
ambitious adj. 有雄心的、宏大的 ambitious plan/goal This is an ambitious roadmap for the next release.
appraisal n. 评估、考核 performance appraisal Annual appraisals determine promotion eligibility.
approve v. 批准、同意 approve a request/proposal The director approved the new hiring plan.
asset n. 资产、优势 valuable asset, company assets Her bilingual skills are a great asset to the team.
assign v. 分配、指派 assign a task/role The tech lead assigned the bug fix to a junior developer.
attain v. 获得、达到 attain a target/goal The sales team attained 120% of their quarterly target.
audit n./v. 审计 internal audit, audit report The finance team conducts an internal audit every year.
acquisition n. 收购、获取 customer acquisition Customer acquisition cost dropped by 20% after the campaign.
advocate v./n. 提倡/拥护者 advocate for change She advocates for better work-life balance policies.

日常与社交

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配 例句
accommodate v. 容纳、迁就 accommodate a request The hotel can accommodate up to 200 guests.
acquaintance n. 熟人 casual acquaintance He is not a close friend — just an acquaintance.
adapt v. 适应 adapt to changes It took me a month to adapt to the new city.
affordable adj. 负担得起的 affordable price/housing We found an affordable apartment near the office.
appetite n. 食欲、兴趣 lose one's appetite I lost my appetite after the long flight.
arrangement n. 安排 make arrangements Let me make arrangements for the dinner party.
awkward adj. 尴尬的 awkward silence/situation There was an awkward silence after his joke.
appreciate v. 感激、欣赏 appreciate your help I really appreciate your help with the presentation.
anxiety n. 焦虑 cause anxiety Public speaking can cause a lot of anxiety for some people.
available adj. 可用的、有空的 available for a meeting Are you available for a quick call this afternoon?
adequate adj. 足够的、适当的 adequate preparation Make sure you have adequate rest before the marathon.

技术相关

词汇 词性 含义 常见搭配 例句
algorithm n. 算法 sorting algorithm, search algorithm The recommendation algorithm needs optimization.
API n. 应用程序接口 RESTful API, call an API We exposed a new API endpoint for user profiles.
architecture n. 架构 system architecture, microservice architecture The team is migrating to a microservice architecture.
authenticate v. 认证、验证 authenticate a user Users must authenticate before accessing the dashboard.
automate v. 自动化 automate testing/deployment We automated the CI/CD pipeline to reduce manual work.
asynchronous adj. 异步的 asynchronous request/call The frontend makes asynchronous calls to fetch data.
abstraction n. 抽象 layer of abstraction Good abstraction hides implementation details from users.
assertion n. 断言 assertion error, test assertion The unit test failed because of a wrong assertion.
array n. 数组 array index, iterate over an array The function accepts an array of user IDs as input.
attribute n. 属性 HTML attribute, data attribute Add a custom data attribute to the DOM element.
aggregate v./n. 聚合 aggregate data, aggregate function We aggregate daily metrics into weekly reports.

易混淆词辨析

词组 区别说明
affect vs. effect affect 是动词「影响」，effect 通常是名词「效果」。The change will affect users. / The effect was noticeable.
assure vs. ensure vs. insure assure「向人保证」，ensure「确保某事发生」，insure「投保」。I assure you / Ensure quality / Insure the package.
adapt vs. adopt adapt「适应」，adopt「采用、收养」。Adapt to change. / Adopt a new strategy.
advice vs. advise advice 名词「建议」，advise 动词「建议」。Give advice. / I advise you to reconsider.
accept vs. except accept「接受」，except「除了」。Accept the offer. / Everyone except Tom attended.
amount vs. number amount 用于不可数名词，number 用于可数名词。A large amount of data. / A large number of users.

高频短语

短语 含义 例句
as soon as possible (ASAP) 尽快 Please send me the report ASAP.
ahead of schedule 提前于计划 The project was delivered ahead of schedule.
across the board 全面地 Salaries were increased across the board.
at stake 处于危险中 Millions of dollars are at stake in this deal.
align with 与……对齐/一致 Our strategy should align with the company vision.
as a whole 总体上 The team performed well as a whole this sprint.
account for 占比、解释 Mobile traffic accounts for 60% of total visits.
at the earliest 最早 The fix will be deployed by Friday at the earliest.
act on 根据……采取行动 We need to act on the feedback from the user survey.
allow for 考虑到、留出余地 Allow for extra time when estimating complex tasks.
as opposed to 而不是 We chose a monorepo as opposed to separate repositories.
all along 一直、始终 The root cause was a typo all along.
