五年级英语 — 一般将来时

知识要点

一般将来时（Simple Future Tense）用来描述将来会发生的动作或存在的状态。学会一般将来时，我们就能用英语谈论计划、打算和预测未来的事情。

一般将来时的两种常用结构：

结构 用法 示例
will + 动词原形 表示预测、临时决定 It will rain tomorrow.
be going to + 动词原形 表示计划、打算 I am going to visit my uncle.

详细讲解

一、will + 动词原形

will用于所有人称，后面直接跟动词原形。

肯定句：主语 + will + 动词原形

主语 例句
I I will help you.
He/She She will come tomorrow.
We We will have a party.
They They will be happy.

否定句：主语 + will not (won't) + 动词原形

I will go. → I won't go.
She will come. → She won't come.

疑问句：Will + 主语 + 动词原形？

Will you come to my party?
— Yes, I will. / No, I won't.

Will it rain tomorrow?
— Maybe. / I think so.

二、be going to + 动词原形

be going to表示事先计划好的或者根据迹象推断将要发生的事。

主语 be动词 例句
I am I am going to study English tonight.
He/She/It is She is going to visit her grandma.
You/We/They are We are going to have a picnic.

否定句：在be动词后加not

I am going to swim. → I am not going to swim.
He is going to play. → He is not (isn't) going to play.

疑问句：把be动词提到主语前面

Are you going to travel this summer?
— Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.

Is she going to buy a new book?
— Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.

三、will与be going to的区别

对比项 will be going to
语气 临时决定或预测 事先计划好的
是否有准备 通常没有提前准备 已经有了计划
示例场景 "天好冷，我去关窗户" "我明天要去动物园"

对话示例：

场景一（临时决定 → 用will）：
A: It's so cold in here.
B: I'll close the window. (我这就去关窗户)

场景二（事先计划 → 用be going to）：
A: What are you going to do this weekend?
B: I'm going to visit the museum. (我打算去参观博物馆)

四、一般将来时的时间标志词

时间标志词 中文意思 例句
tomorrow 明天 I will go to school tomorrow.
next week/month/year 下周/下个月/明年 We are going to travel next month.
this afternoon/evening 今天下午/晚上 She will call you this evening.
soon 很快 The bus will come soon.
in the future 在将来 Robots will help us in the future.

五、对话与写作练习

对话一：谈论周末计划

A: What are you going to do this weekend?
B: I'm going to go hiking with my dad.
A: Sounds fun! Where will you go?
B: We will go to the mountain near our city.
A: Will you take some food?
B: Yes, we are going to prepare sandwiches and fruit.
A: Have a great time!
B: Thank you! I'm sure it will be wonderful.

对话二：谈论未来职业

A: What do you want to be when you grow up?
B: I'm going to be a doctor.
A: That's great! Why?
B: Because I want to help sick people. I think it will be meaningful.
A: I believe you will be a wonderful doctor!
B: Thank you! What about you?
A: I think I will be a teacher.

练习与思考

练习一：选择will或be going to

  1. Look at those dark clouds! It ___ (rain).
  2. "I'm thirsty." "I ___ (get) you some water."
  3. We ___ (have) a class meeting next Friday. Our teacher told us.
  4. I think robots ___ (do) housework in the future.

练习二：句型转换

将下列句子变成否定句和疑问句：

  1. She will visit her grandma tomorrow.
  2. They are going to play football this afternoon.
  3. I will be a scientist in the future.

练习三：写作练习

以"My Plan for the Summer Holiday"为题，写一段话（至少6句），描述你暑假打算做什么。使用一般将来时。

提示词：travel, visit, read, learn, play, swim

思考题：

"I will go to Beijing tomorrow."和"I am going to Beijing tomorrow."有什么区别？哪种说法更表明你已经做好了准备？

拓展阅读

一般将来时是英语中谈论未来的基本工具。在日常交流中，我们经常需要谈论计划和预测。学会使用will和be going to，你就能：

  • 制定学习计划："I'm going to study math every evening."
  • 预测天气："I think it will be sunny."
  • 表达愿望："I will work hard to get better grades."

有趣的文化知识：西方国家的小朋友在新年时会制定"New Year's Resolutions"（新年决心），用will和be going to来表达新一年的目标和计划。

小结

本节课我们学习了一般将来时的两种结构：will + 动词原形和be going to + 动词原形。will常用于临时决定和预测，be going to常用于事先计划好的事情。掌握了一般将来时，配合之前学过的一般现在时和一般过去时，你就能用英语表达过去、现在和未来三种时间概念了。
