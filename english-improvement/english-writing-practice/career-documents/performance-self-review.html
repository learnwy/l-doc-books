绩效自评

核心概念

绩效自评（performance self-review）是展示你的工作价值和职业成长的关键文档。在多数科技公司中，自评是晋升和加薪决策的重要输入。写好自评的核心挑战是在 self-promotion（自我推销）和 authenticity（真实性）之间找到平衡——既不能过度谦虚，也不能夸大其词。

绩效自评与简历的写作有相似之处，但目标受众和侧重点不同：

核心原则是 Make your manager's job easy — 你的自评应该让经理能直接引用你的内容来为你争取好评。


成就描述的 IMPACT 框架
绩效自评中的成就描述推荐使用 IMPACT 框架：Initiative（主动性）、Method（方法）、People（协作）、Achievement（成果）、Connection（关联）、Takeaway（收获）。


完整示例：




Initiative: Identified that our API response times were degrading as user base grew, proactively proposing a caching strategy before it became a P0 issue.


Method: Designed and implemented a multi-tier caching architecture (L1: in-memory, L2: Redis) with intelligent cache invalidation based on data access patterns.


People: Collaborated with the data team on access pattern analysis and worked with SRE on Redis cluster sizing. Mentored a junior engineer who implemented the cache warming strategy.


Achievement: Reduced p95 API latency from 450ms to 120ms (73% improvement). Saved $8K/month in compute costs by reducing database load by 60%.


Connection: This directly supported the company's Q3 OKR of "Improve platform reliability to 99.95% uptime" and unblocked the sales team's enterprise client expansion.


Takeaway: Deepened my expertise in distributed caching and learned valuable lessons about cache invalidation strategies in eventually-consistent systems.

与级别框架对齐
大多数科技公司有明确的工程师级别框架（leveling rubric），自评中的每个成就都应明确对齐到框架中的某个维度。


常见的级别维度及自评对齐示例：


Technical Complexity（技术复杂度）：




Architected the event-driven notification system that processes 2M+ events daily across 5 microservices. Made key design decisions around message ordering guarantees and exactly-once delivery semantics that were validated in production with zero data loss over 6 months.




Scope of Impact（影响范围）：




The caching infrastructure I built was adopted by 3 other teams beyond my own, becoming the standard caching solution across the platform organization. This cross-team adoption was not originally planned — I documented the solution and presented it at an engineering all-hands, which generated organic interest.




Leadership & Influence（领导力与影响力）：




Led the RFC process for our API versioning strategy, a contentious topic that affected 6 teams. Facilitated 3 design review sessions, incorporated feedback from 12 engineers, and drove the group to consensus on a solution that balanced backward compatibility with development velocity.




Mentorship & Growth（指导与成长）：




Mentored 2 junior engineers this cycle. One of them (Alex) successfully delivered their first independently owned project — the rate limiting service — which I helped scope and reviewed all design decisions for. Alex has since been promoted to mid-level.




在自评中，使用框架的原话来描述你的成就。如果公司框架中写 drives technical decisions for their team，你的自评中就应该出现 drove technical decisions for the team, specifically...。


成长领域的诚实表达
自评中的 Areas for Growth 部分容易陷入两个极端：要么完全不写（显得缺乏自知之明），要么写得过于严重（给自己减分）。正确的做法是选择 1-2 个真实的改进领域，并展示你已经在行动。


好的成长领域写法：




Area: Cross-team communication during large projects.


Context: During the Q3 API migration, I focused heavily on execution and didn't invest enough in proactive stakeholder communication. This led to some surprise when timelines shifted, and I had to do reactive communication rather than keeping everyone in the loop from the start.


Action taken: I've started sending weekly stakeholder updates for all projects I lead. I've also set up bi-weekly sync meetings with the product and design leads to ensure alignment.


Goal for next cycle: Receive positive feedback from at least 2 cross-functional partners on communication during the Q1 project.

自评的语气与常用句式
自评的语气应该是 confident but not arrogant（自信但不自大）。以下是常用的句式模板：


描述成就时：


    

  • I'm proud of... — 表达自豪但不夸张
    • 

  • A highlight of this cycle was... — 突出重点
    • 

  • One of my most impactful contributions was... — 强调影响力
    • 

  • I took the initiative to... — 展示主动性
    • 



描述协作时：


    

  • I partnered with [team/person] to... — 展示协作
    • 

  • Working closely with [team], I... — 强调跨团队能力
    • 

  • I facilitated alignment between... — 展示协调能力
    • 



描述成长时：


    

  • I've been actively working on... — 展示行动
    • 

  • An area I'm focused on improving is... — 正面表达不足
    • 

  • I've made progress on... and plan to continue... — 展示持续性
    • 



避免使用的表达：


    

  • I just did my job. — 过度贬低自己的贡献
    • 

  • It was nothing special. — 削弱成就的价值
    • 

  • Anyone could have done this. — 否定个人能力
    • 

  • I'm the best engineer on the team. — 傲慢且不合适
    • 



自评的数据收集与准备
优秀的自评需要提前准备，而不是在截止日期前匆忙回忆。以下是系统化的数据收集策略：


持续记录——每周用 10 分钟记录本周的关键成就：




Week of Mar 11: Completed caching layer design. Reduced p95 latency by 73%. PR #2345 merged after 2 rounds of review.




收集他人反馈——保存 Slack 中同事的正面反馈、邮件中的感谢、代码审查中的 [Praise] 评论：




"Thanks for the thorough RFC, Alex. This saved us a lot of back-and-forth." — David, Slack #platform-eng, Mar 15




量化工具——利用 GitHub 统计（PR 数量、代码审查数量）、Jira 统计（完成的 story points）、监控面板（性能指标改善）等客观数据。

小结
    

  • 成就描述使用 IMPACT 框架，确保每项成就都有量化结果和目标关联
    • 

  • 将成就对齐到公司的级别框架维度，使用框架原话
    • 

  • 核心原则是 Make your manager's job easy
    • 

  • 成长领域选 1-2 个真实的改进点，展示已采取的行动和下周期目标
    • 

  • 语气 confident but not arrogant，使用 I'm proud of 而非 I'm the best
    • 

  • 避免 I just did my job 等自我贬低的表达
    • 

  • 收集数据和同事反馈作为成就的佐证
