会议纪要与行动项

核心概念

会议纪要（meeting notes）和行动项（action items）是将会议讨论转化为实际成果的桥梁。一场没有纪要的会议等于没开——参与者很快就会忘记讨论了什么、决定了什么、谁该做什么。好的会议纪要不是逐字记录，而是 decisions + actions + context 的结构化提炼。

会议纪要与会议录音/转写有本质区别：

核心原则是 Capture decisions, not discussions — 记录结论而非过程。


会议纪要的标准结构
一份高质量的会议纪要应遵循 DAFA 结构：Details（基本信息）、Agenda & Discussion（议程与讨论）、Final Decisions（最终决策）、Action Items（行动项）。


完整的会议纪要模板：




Meeting Notes: v3.0 Launch Go/No-Go
Date: March 27, 2024, 2:00-2:45 PM PST Attendees: Lisa (facilitator), David, Sarah, Alex, Director Chen Absent: Tom (on PTO)




Agenda & Key Discussion Points
1. Launch Readiness Review


Lisa presented the launch readiness scorecard. All 12 checklist items are green except partner communication (yellow — 85% of partners notified, remaining 15% scheduled for today).


2. Risk Assessment


David raised a concern about the load testing results — p99 latency is 280ms vs the 200ms target. After discussion, the team agreed this is acceptable for launch given that p95 is 180ms and the p99 spike is caused by cold starts that will decrease after warmup.


3. Rollback Strategy


Alex confirmed the rollback plan: feature flag kill switch (< 1 min) + full revert (< 5 min). Director Chen approved the plan.

  2. ✅ P99 latency accepted at 280ms — will optimize post-launch in Q2

  3. ✅ Rollback plan approved — feature flag as primary mechanism

行动项的 SMART 写法
行动项是会议纪要中最重要的部分。模糊的行动项等于没有行动项。每个行动项都应符合 SMART 标准。


模糊的行动项：




    

  • Look into the performance issue
    • 

  • Update the docs
    • 

  • Follow up with the team
    • 





SMART 行动项：




    

  • David: Profile and optimize the p99 latency for the /api/users endpoint. Target: reduce from 280ms to <200ms. Deadline: April 15.
    • 

  • Sarah: Update API documentation for the three new v3.0 endpoints (auth, users, billing). Deadline: March 28 EOD.
    • 

  • Lisa: Schedule a 30-min retro with the launch team to capture lessons learned. Deadline: April 5.
    • 





每个行动项必须包含四个要素：






要素
含义
示例





Who
负责人（一个人）
David




What
具体任务
Profile and optimize p99 latency




How much
可衡量的目标
Reduce from 280ms to <200ms




When
截止日期
April 15





特别注意 Who — 每个行动项只能有一个负责人。如果写 David and Sarah，就没有人真正负责。如果需要协作，写法应该是：




David (with support from Sarah): Profile and optimize p99 latency.




行动项的状态追踪通常在下次会议的开头进行。在 Slack 中可以用 emoji 标注状态：




📋 Action Items from March 27 Go/No-Go: ✅ #1 Partner notifications — completed by Lisa ✅ #2 Smoke test — completed by David ✅ #3 Incident runbook — completed by Alex 🔄 #4 Launch announcement — in progress (Director Chen) ⏳ #5 Brief Tom — pending (Tom returns Monday)




不同会议类型的纪要侧重
不同类型的会议需要不同侧重的纪要：


决策会议（Decision Meeting）— 重点记录选项对比和最终决策：




Topic: Choose between PostgreSQL and DynamoDB for the new service


Options discussed:


    

  • PostgreSQL: Familiar to the team, strong consistency, complex queries supported
    • 

  • DynamoDB: Better scalability, lower ops overhead, eventual consistency acceptable for this use case
    • 



Decision: DynamoDB — primary driver is reduced operational overhead, which aligns with our goal of reducing infrastructure team burden.


Dissenting view: David preferred PostgreSQL for query flexibility. Team agreed to revisit if complex query needs emerge.




记录 dissenting view 是一种最佳实践——它证明了决策经过了充分讨论，也为未来可能的重新评估提供了参考。


头脑风暴会议（Brainstorm）— 重点记录产生的想法和后续评估计划：




Ideas generated (not prioritized):


    

  1. Server-side rendering for initial page load
    2. 

  2. Edge caching with CDN for static assets
    3. 

  3. Image lazy loading with blur-up placeholders
    4. 

  4. Bundle splitting by route
    5. 



Next step: Alex will create evaluation criteria and score each idea by impact/effort. Review in next week's planning meeting.




回顾会议（Retrospective）— 重点记录改进项和负责人：




What went well: 🎉


    

  • Cross-team communication was excellent during the incident
    • 

  • Rollback executed in under 2 minutes
    • 



What could improve: 🔧


    

  • Monitoring alerts triggered 15 minutes after the issue started
    • 

  • No runbook existed for this specific failure mode
    • 



Action items:


    

  • Alex: Add p99 latency alert with 5-min threshold (Apr 5)
    • 

  • Sarah: Create runbook for database connection pool exhaustion (Apr 10)
    • 





纪要发送与跟进规范
会议纪要应在会议结束后 2 小时内发送。延迟发送会降低纪要的可靠性——你的记忆会随时间衰减。


发送纪要的邮件/Slack 格式：




Subject: [Meeting Notes] v3.0 Launch Go/No-Go — March 27


Hi all,


Here are the notes from today's go/no-go meeting. TL;DR: We're a go for March 29 launch.


[Full notes below or linked to shared doc]


Key action items (please confirm by replying):


    

  • @Lisa: Complete partner notifications (Mar 28)
    • 

  • @David: Final smoke test (Mar 29, 8am)
    • 

  • @Alex: Incident runbook (Mar 28)
    • 



Please review and let me know if I've missed or misrepresented anything.


Thanks, Lisa




关键技巧：


    

  • TL;DR 在开头提供一句话摘要
    • 

  • 行动项重复 在正文中再次列出，方便收件人快速确认
    • 

  • 请求确认 — please confirm by replying 确保负责人知悉自己的任务
    • 

  • 纠正邀请 — let me know if I've missed anything 确保准确性
    • 



对于缺席者（如上例中的 Tom），单独发一条简要说明：




Hi Tom, hope you're enjoying your PTO! Quick summary of today's go/no-go: we're launching v3.0 on March 29. Full notes are [here]. One action item for you: [details]. No rush — we'll catch up when you're back Monday.




小结
    

  • 会议纪要使用 DAFA 结构：Details、Agenda、Final Decisions、Action Items
    • 

  • 核心原则是 Capture decisions, not discussions
    • 

  • 行动项符合 SMART 标准：Who + What + How much + When
    • 

  • 每个行动项只有一个负责人，协作用 with support from 表达
    • 

  • 决策会议记录 dissenting view，回顾会议记录改进项和负责人
    • 

  • 会议结束后 2 小时内发送纪要，开头放 TL;DR
    • 

  • 行动项在邮件正文中重复列出，请求负责人确认
    • 

  • 为缺席者单独发送简要说明
    •
